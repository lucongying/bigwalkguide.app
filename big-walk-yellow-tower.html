Unlock the Yellow Tower and open the Big Tunnel

The Big Tunnel is the map-wide underground shortcut — those colorful drain-like openings you keep past are all connected, and the Yellow Tower is what unlocks them. Solve puzzles for 5 red gourds, place them at the top, run the yellow key through 5 cutters, and every tunnel entrance opens. Walk through any door to earn the Big Tunnel achievement, grab the last hidden radio, and shortcut your way toward the Blue Tower and endgame. It's pitch black inside, but a torch waits at every entrance.

Before you start

  • Ideally finish the Green Tower first — the chairlift reaches the Yellow Tower far faster than walking over the mountains
  • At least 2 players — these puzzles are heavy on cross-room communication
  • Know the key-cutting mechanic
  • Bring a walkie-talkie (or megaphone) and a light source; several puzzles require passing positions/sounds between separate rooms

How to solve it

  1. 01

    Take the chairlift to the Yellow Tower (3310, 1560)

    The Yellow Tower is in the northern part of the map. Back at the Green Tower's chairlift station, face the tallest mountain and take the left-side line to the Yellow route; drop off and it's a short walk. You can also walk from the start area, but it's a long trek through the mountains. The tower's top has a 5-slot receiver — learn that endpoint first so you always know where to bank each gourd. Stow spare gear at the tower and carry just one light plus one communication tool onto the beach.

  2. 02

    Solve any 5 puzzles for 5 red gourds

    Red gourds are interchangeable — any 5 island puzzle rewards work, not one fixed set. The most convenient ones near the tower are listed below (with a spare option beyond the five). Don't haul your whole gear pile down to the beach at once: bank the first 2 gourds at the tower as a checkpoint, learn the return route, then commit to the remaining 3.

  3. 03

    Place them at the top for the yellow key

    Slot all five red gourds into the receiver at the top of the Yellow Tower, one per slot, each lighting a cell; once the fifth lights, the key pops out behind the panel. Even one empty slot stops it. What you get here is a blank key — it won't work yet.

  4. 04

    Run the key through 5 cutters to shape it

    Start at the first cutter beside the tower door; each cutter shows an arrow to the next. The Yellow set runs roughly: ① by the tower door → ② on the nearby cliff → ③ the one facing the Black Tower → ④ pointing to a rock on the right (not the tunnel) → ⑤ beside the Yellow Tunnel entrance. Keep one permanent key carrier and one light-holder so the key can't slide down a slope and vanish; if it drops, recover it before moving on.

  5. 05

    Insert the key at the Yellow Tunnel (3500, 1620)

    The Yellow Tunnel entrance is southeast of the tower: head down the south slope and follow the rock face. It sits directly across from a blue building that holds a backpack — grab it while you're there. Slot the finished key into the terminal on the right of the entrance and every big tunnel across the map opens.

  6. 06

    Step through the tunnel door for the achievement

    Crossing a tunnel door is what counts — standing outside or only finishing the 5th cutter doesn't. Going in unlocks the Big Tunnel achievement, grants the final hidden radio (channel #5, at the DJ booth inside), and is the shortcut toward the Blue Tower and endgame. It's completely dark, so take the torch at the entrance; then follow the colored route markers — head toward Blue if the train is next.

Yellow Tower puzzles (complete any 5)

  • Colored Pegs Board3370, 1490
    Find 36 colored pegs (9 each of four colors). Yellow pegs are near trees/bushes, green near rocks/cliffs (one green peg is only reachable through an orange side tunnel), red around the red stage area, and blue pegs by climbing onto the rails. Place them all on the wheel in any order. This is the slowest tower search — about 1–1.5 in-game days for two players, so do it in daylight.
  • Microphone3400, 1230
    Enter the beige-and-red archway. One player sits with headphones (other sounds are muffled) while the other speaks into each of the 6 mics in turn; the seated player judges which is loudest, signals with raised arms, and the speaker presses that mic's button. Don't reuse a mic, since the loudest one can shift between rounds.
  • Sound Booth3200, 1260
    In the beige-and-red striped building, top-left of the area. Outside, several yellow speakers play different sounds beside 5 booths with hanging spots; a locked room holds 5 more booths, each playing a sound that matches one outside speaker. The locked player listens and describes each over the intercom (instrument, whether the pitch rises/falls/holds, and whether it's short, repeated, or sustained); the outside player finds the matching speaker and hangs it in the right booth. Avoid private nicknames — say something like 'three short clicks then a low tone'.
  • Blue Stage Buttons3410, 1590
    On a dark blue-and-black stage, southeast of the peg board. One player is locked in the control room watching 7 lights (numbered top-left to right); the other works a mirrored button panel: 1↔3, 2↔2, 3↔1, 4↔7, 5↔6, 6↔5, 7↔4. The caller converts the light to its mirrored button, then signals with raised-arm counts.
  • Aerial Chair Symbols3570, 1600
    A crescent chair rides an aerial rail. One platform holds 9 symbol pieces while the other board shows a 5-symbol sequence (the sequence is fixed for the puzzle, but verify the board in your own session). Ride the chair to ferry the correct pieces across, set them on the board beside the black screen, then press the red button under it. Showing each piece from the start platform and confirming with a telescope is slow — backpacks let everyone carry an extra piece and cut the trips, but unload before riding back.
  • Button Room (spare)3290, 1670
    East of the tower behind the chairlift station, two cliff-side buildings sit opposite each other — one a light room, one a button room, with a yellow cage between them for shouting. The player in the light room describes which light is lit using simple vocabulary (left wall, back wall, curved wall, and the count); the other presses the matching red button. Swap this in if you keep stalling on a communication puzzle.

Why it won't work

The Yellow Tower won't give a key?

Your 5 red gourds aren't all placed. Check every slot at the top — even one empty stops the key.

The tunnel won't open?

Either the key isn't fully cut, or you're inserting it in the wrong place — it goes in the slot on the right of the Yellow Tunnel entrance (3500, 1620), not the tower.

Not enough red gourds?

Red gourds are interchangeable. Any island puzzle reward works, so solve puzzles elsewhere to reach 5.

The key vanished after a fall?

Stop and search the slope directly below the carrier — the key slides farther than the player. Don't kick or throw it, and check who's holding it after any drop.

The highlighted button keeps changing?

Someone on the answer side is pressing extra buttons, shifting the active target. Stop all input, let the caller read the new target, and let only one player press.

The tunnel is open but the Blue side is still blocked?

The tunnel is transport — it won't fill the Blue receiver's gourds or cut the blue key. Exit toward the Blue beacon and run the Blue Tower chain.

Too dark inside the tunnel?

Every entrance has a torch — grab one before heading in. A flashlight or glow orb works too; keep the light and key carriers together.
