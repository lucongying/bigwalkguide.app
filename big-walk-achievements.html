All 12 achievements, and how to get them

Steam has 12 achievements (PS5 has 13 with the Platinum). Most are checkpoints you pass anyway, but a few need a deliberate detour. Two patterns trip people up: several only count a final action — crossing instead of just opening, wearing instead of holding, entering instead of standing outside, riding instead of reaching — and Big Game can't pop until you have finished the same save's full cleanup. (Guides disagree on the collectibles' name: this guide calls them gourds, others call them blorbs.)

Full achievement list

  • Big Walk
    Finish the starting area's four co-op puzzles and cut the opening key, then cross the lowered drawbridge to the main-island side. It pops when you actually walk across — opening the bridge on its own is not the trigger. Story, nearly guaranteed.
  • Big View3560, 1425
    After the Red Tower, use the cut red key at the large staircase by the tutorial area and walk into the physical Map Room (the room with the big island model on the floor, and the foldable map + compass on the table). Picking up the portable map is not the trigger — you have to be inside the room. Story.
  • Big Sit3950, 1950
    Finish the Green Tower, power up the chairlift station (3950, 1950), wait for a seat to circulate and board it. Reaching the station or standing beside an idle chair doesn't count — you have to be riding a moving chair. Story.
  • Big Ride3380, 1740
    Finish the Blue Tower, start the monorail at the station (3380, 1740), wait for a carriage to pass and climb aboard while it's moving. Reaching the station, seeing the train, or standing on the platform won't do it — ride a moving carriage. Story.
  • Big Tunnel3500, 1620
    After the Yellow Tower, cut and use the yellow key, then walk fully inside the tunnel (3500, 1620). Reaching the entrance, seeing the door open, or standing at the threshold doesn't count — the character has to be inside the tunnel. Story.
  • Big Wall
    Finish the Black Obelisk, get the six-gourd Black Key, use it on the gate at the far-west wall, then keep walking past it to the far side. Finding the gate or arriving with the key isn't enough — you need to be beyond the wall. Late-game.
  • Big Pack
    Find a backpack in one of the blue spiral structures (these are mostly early-game) and have a teammate attach it to your back. Holding it in your hands isn't wearing it — the achievement needs it visibly on your back. Optional, and it frees a hand for carrying.
  • Big Help
    Equip the hip belt from a blue spiral structure (these are mostly later ones), then attach a compatible item — the folded portable map is the easiest test — to the belt slot. Just carrying the belt isn't enough; an item has to be hung from the equipped belt. Optional; a belt lets you hang a tool, radio or walkie-talkie on your hip.
  • Big Climb
    Ride the chairlift one station west of the one that unlocks it (that stop is on the island's tallest mountain, marked by an orange structure at the summit), then climb the rest of the way on foot and stand on top. It's separate from Big Sit — Big Sit is the ride, Big Climb is the peak — and each player has to reach the summit individually. Optional, needs a detour.
  • Big Makeover
    This needs the Yellow tunnels open first. Head into the tunnel network near the red/blue connection close to the rave, cross the green bridge, take the winding red stairs down into a purple space that holds a second salon, and have a teammate paint you with a metallic color (gold, silver or bronze). Reaching the area isn't the trigger — the Shiny paint job is. Optional, and easy to miss.
  • Big Goodbye
    After the Wall, make the long walk to the giant sphere, complete the co-op rooms inside, exit the far side, and pass through the final turnstile and the white door to finish the standard ending. Big Goodbye is normal completion, and it always comes before Big Game. Hidden.
  • Big Game
    Load the same save after the credits — don't start a new world. Collect the gourds left over beyond the ones already used on keys (the eight additional red ones, plus the purple challenge ones behind the far-east chairlift station), hand all 15 in at the 15-slot green dome near the train station / Map Room to raise the white key, then take it to the split-open black sphere in the tutorial area, insert it and go down to the hidden door. Red and purple gourds are interchangeable when handing in. Requires the standard ending first. Hidden.

The patterns that trip people up

The final-action rule

Several achievements don't pop on reaching the right place — they need one last action. Crossing instead of just opening (Big Walk), wearing instead of holding (Big Pack), entering instead of standing outside (Big Tunnel), riding instead of reaching (Big Sit, Big Ride), passing beyond instead of arriving (Big Wall). When one of these looks stuck, check that you did the final action, not the setup.

The pairs that look the same but aren't

Big Sit is the chairlift ride, while Big Climb is the peak you climb to afterwards — and each player has to stand on top individually. Likewise Big Goodbye is the normal ending and Big Game is the true one: two different states, so Big Goodbye popping without Big Game just means you still have cleanup.

An order that never breaks progression

Natural route: Big Walk → Big View → Big Sit → Big Tunnel → Big Ride → Big Wall → Big Goodbye. Grab the equipment ones the moment you're at a backpack or belt (Big Pack, Big Help). Leave the detours for later: Big Climb once you're on the high route, Big Makeover once the tunnels are open, and Big Game after the ending plus cleanup.

Why Big Makeover is the one people miss

On PS5 it's rarer than finishing the game. It's tucked away in a part of the tunnel network nobody passes through by accident, so plenty of groups beat the game without ever finding it. If you're chasing the platinum, clear it the moment the tunnels open instead of backtracking later.

What the rarity numbers tell you

PS5 completion data shows where the drop-off is: crossing the drawbridge sits around 50% of owners, Big Wall 4.2%, Big Goodbye 4.0%, Big Makeover 2.4%, Big Game 1.1%, and the platinum 0.9%. Far fewer groups finish the endings than you'd expect — most are still clearing towers.

Why it won't work

Which are easy to miss?

Big Climb, Big Makeover, and Big Game need deliberate effort off the main path; the rest unlock naturally through progression. Of those, Big Makeover is the most commonly missed — it's hidden in the tunnel network and isn't on the way to the ending.

Big Pack didn't pop when I picked up a backpack?

You need to wear it, not carry it. Have a teammate attach the backpack to your back — it has to be visibly equipped before the achievement registers.

Big View didn't pop when I grabbed the portable map?

The portable map is not the trigger. Big View comes from entering the physical Map Room — the room with the island model — after completing the Red Tower and using the key at the staircase by the tutorial area.

Big Help didn't pop after I equipped the belt?

The belt alone is incomplete. Hang a compatible item off it — the folded portable map is the cleanest test — and leave it attached until the achievement registers.

Big Sit / Big Ride won't pop at the station?

Both need a moving vehicle, not a stopped one. For Big Sit, board a circulating chair after the chairlift is live. For Big Ride, climb onto a carriage while it's moving — standing on the platform or reaching the station doesn't count.

Big Goodbye popped but Big Game didn't?

That's expected — they're two different completion states. Load the same save after the credits, collect the leftover gourds, hand 15 in at the green dome to raise the white key, and use it in the tutorial area to finish the full route.

Big Makeover didn't pop when I reached the Shiny area?

Finding the hidden salon is not the final trigger. Complete the Shiny interaction — have a teammate paint you with a metallic color — before you leave. Getting to the location alone isn't enough.

What about the PS5 Platinum?

PS5 adds one Big Trophy Platinum — unlock the other 12 to get it. There's no separate work for it; it's awarded automatically once the 12 are done.
