About this guide

An unofficial Big Walk fan guide — coordinates, walkthroughs and puzzle solutions, written and tested by players.

Who we are

bigwalkguide.app is an unofficial fan guide made by players of Big Walk. We are not the developers, the publisher, or affiliated in any way with House House or Panic — we're just a group of fans who love the game and wanted to put every answer we kept searching for in one place.

What we do

Big Walk drops 2–12 players onto an open island with no quest markers and no map, so this guide collects the scattered answers into a clear, searchable library:

  • Tower walkthroughs — every coloured tower, and the order that makes them easiest
  • True-ending route — the seven purple puzzles and how they unlock the real ending
  • Collectible coordinates — backpacks, bell bags, radios and more, indexed by the game's own (X, Y) system
  • Puzzle solutions — key cutting, the 4166/1899 coordinate, and every spot players get stuck
  • FAQ and achievements — dozens of common questions and all 12 achievements

Our principles

Every answer comes from real playthroughs and community research, and we correct the data as the game updates. If you spot an error or something out of date, tell us via the Contact page and we'll fix it as soon as we can.
