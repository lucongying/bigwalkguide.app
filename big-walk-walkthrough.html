The main route, stage by stage

Big Walk is open-world, so you can hit the towers in any order. There is still a natural spine underneath: drop the drawbridge, then use each tower to open the map room, the chairlifts, the tunnels and the trains, until the Black Tower opens and the sphere carries you to the ending. That climb is laid out here, with what you earn at each step.

How your progress works

Every tower is a key, and every key is a chain of puzzles

Each coloured tower asks for a set of gourd-shaped prizes. They come from the puzzles scattered around that tower, one each. Slot them into the console on the roof and the tower releases its key. Most towers want five prizes; the Black Tower wants six.

Cut the key before it opens anything

This is the easiest way to get stuck. A key straight from a tower is not ready to use, it is a rough shape. You run it through a string of key cutters, following the arrow after each cut, until it is fully shaped. Only then will it open the door, the map room, the station or the gate it belongs to.

Each tower buys you a way to get around

The reward is not just a trophy, it is your transport. Red opens the Map Room with its 2D map. Green powers the chairlifts. Yellow switches on the tunnels. Blue starts the trains. Black opens the wall that leads to the ending.

The route, stage by stage

0 · The opening, the drawbridge

Solve the four starting puzzles (Blue Room, Green Structure, Mechanical Arm, Cave) for four figurines, cut the key through five cutters, and lower the red drawbridge. That opens the rest of the island. You will also spot a locked blue doorway, which is the Map Room, waiting for the Red Tower.

1 · Red Tower, Map Room

Five puzzles around the red cone give five red gourds. Place them on the roof, cut the key, and the big blue hall opens. Inside are a huge 3D island map, a foldable 2D map, and tools like flashlights, laser pointers and a compass. The 2D map is the most useful thing in the game, there is no other built-in map — it zooms and lets you sketch sections, but won't show your live position, so you still need the GPS or a compass to pin yourself. Stepping into the room for the first time also unlocks the Big View achievement; carry the map in a backpack, space is limited.

2 · Green Tower, Chairlift

Solve five puzzles over by the mountains, the 4166/1899 coordinate puzzle is one of them, for five gourd-shaped Heads. Cut the key and the chairlift comes alive. Five stops span the island end to end, and riding one also unlocks the Big Sit achievement.

3 · Yellow Tower, Tunnels

Five more puzzles give the yellow key and switch on the underground tunnels. These drain-like openings under the towers are a dark shortcut network, with a flashlight waiting at each entrance. Clearing Yellow also opens the fastest road to the Black Tower and the hidden radio.

4 · Blue Tower, Train

Solve five blue puzzles, cut the key, and start the monorail. The trains loop the island and stop by each tower, turning a long walk into a short ride. Worth doing early even if it is not first.

5 · Black Tower, the normal ending (Big Goodbye)

Once the four coloured towers are done, the Black Obelisk unlocks on its own. It wants six prizes. Cut its special key, take it to the gate on the far west, then cross to the black sphere. Clear the rooms inside, wave goodbye at the sign, and the credits roll. That is Big Goodbye.

6 · True ending, seven purple puzzles (Big Game)

After the first ending you unlock seven harder purple puzzles, reworked versions of challenges you have already beaten. Finish all seven plus anything you skipped, drop the last gourds at the green dome near the train station for the white key, then use it on the cracked black ball back by the start to open the secret room. Read the messages inside for the true ending.

Recommended order

Red tower first

You spawn close to it, and the 2D map it rewards makes everything after easier. The game nudges you here on purpose.

Green or Blue next

Both hand you fast travel. The chairlift and the train are the two comfiest ways across the island.

Yellow whenever you like

It is the farthest tower, and the tunnels are handy but not essential, so it can slide later in the session.

Black tower last

It only opens after the other four, and it is the straight line to the ending.

Why it won't work

Why will my key not open the door?

It is not cut yet. A fresh key is a rough shape, so run it through the cutter chain, following the arrows, until it is finished.

No map and I am lost. Where do I start?

Make the Red Tower your first real goal. Its Map Room holds the only 2D map in the game plus the tools you need to plan the rest.

The tunnels are pitch black. How do I get through?

Every tunnel entrance has a flashlight. Use it, and remember the tunnels are a shortcut network, not a main road, so a quick look at the map room map lines up the two ends.

Is the true ending just the normal one plus a few extras?

It is the normal ending plus every puzzle in the game. The seven purple ones only appear after the first ending, so the white key is a post-game reward.

Does everyone in the group earn the credit?

Progress only counts for whoever personally rides, enters or crosses. Do the tower, chairlift and ending beats together so nobody misses out.

Do I need 100% completion for the normal ending?

No. Big Goodbye only needs the main route: four colored towers, the Black Tower, the Wall, the black sphere, crossing the water, then Wave Goodbye and enter. 100% is for Big Game.

Where are the purple puzzles?

They only unlock after the normal ending, in the far-east chairlift / Challenge Ridge area, behind a purple tunnel.

Is the true ending just red gourds?

No. The true ending needs 8 red gourds + 7 purple gourds — 15 total — all delivered to the green dome building near the train station / Map Room.

Can I keep playing after the normal ending?

Yes. The normal ending isn't a hard stop and doesn't lock you out. Continue the same save: the black sphere stays broken, the 7 purple puzzles unlock, and once you clear skipped normal puzzles and deliver all 15 gourds you can chase the true ending.

The white key won't appear.

The white key is a completeness test — if it hasn't risen, something is still outstanding. Most likely one purple puzzle is unfinished, a normal puzzle was skipped before the normal ending, a challenge was started but never truly finished, or a reward was never delivered to its slot. Go back to the Map Room and sweep the whole island rather than waiting at the spawn point.

I finished all 7 purple puzzles but still no true ending.

The purple puzzles are only part of 100%. Normal puzzles skipped before the normal ending and the 15-gourd delivery also count. The real check: has the white key risen? If it has, the world is complete; if not, something's still missing.

Where exactly do I use the white key?

Not on any tower. Return to the tutorial / spawn area and insert it into the hidden door's keyhole on the wall where the cracked black sphere sits.

I waved but the ending didn't continue.

Wave Goodbye and the entrance button are two separate steps. If you waved and the door didn't respond, go back and press the entrance button again before retracing your route. The keyboard prompt for the wave is Q or E.

What's the small building across the water called?

The game doesn't give it an official name, so it's usually just called the 'ending building' or 'the little house across the water' — the one you enter for the normal ending.

Can I keep playing after the true ending?

Yes. After the Big Game completion screen, the game still offers a Continue option, so you can keep wandering the same save, collect achievements, or replay challenges.
