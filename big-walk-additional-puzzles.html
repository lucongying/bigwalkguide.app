Do not miss these 8 red gourds after the ending

Big Goodbye is not the finish line; Big Game is. It needs 8 red gourds + 7 purple gourds, 15 in total, delivered to the green dome for the white key. Several of those 8 red gourds come from leftover puzzles you will skip on the way through, or that only surface under specific conditions, scattered around the towers and the endgame ridge. Each of the 8 that guides usually list is explained below, with a note where it is the same puzzle you already met on a tower page — come back after the ending to top up your red gourds.

Before you start

  • Already cleared the normal Big Goodbye ending (Black Key → Wall → black sphere)
  • Continue on the same save — the sphere stays broken open and the true-ending content unlocks
  • Picked up the foldable 2D map and compass from the Map Room (most puzzles rely on it to locate things)
  • At least two players — most of these need coordinated cross-distance work, so agree on a voice channel first

How to solve it

  1. 01

    Return to the Map Room and look for empty poles

    The 7 purple puzzles are not the whole picture. First thing after the ending, go back to the Map Room and scan the 3D map: any flag pole with no flag is a challenge you did not truly finish or a red gourd you missed. Walking up and stopping does not count — finish every one and the white key will rise.

  2. 02

    Clear all 7 purple puzzles

    The purple puzzles are at the far-east Challenge Ridge, behind the purple tunnel, one purple gourd each. Harder than their main-line versions; solutions are on each purple puzzle's page. While you line up 7 purple gourds, sweep the additional red gourds at the same time.

  3. 03

    Track down these 8 additional red gourds

    These 8 are the main source of gaps. The Green Flare, Seaside Counting and Heavy Golf Ball are the same mechanics as the ones you saw on the green / blue tower pages — those pages explain the core mechanic, here we look at the instance you return to to top up red gourds.

  4. 04

    Deliver all 15 gourds → white key → true ending

    The green dome sits near the train station / Map Room (the blue music-note icon on the folding map) with exactly 15 slots. Fill all 8 red + 7 purple, and the white key rises at the start; use it on the hidden door in the wall with the cracked black sphere, read the final message, and you unlock the Big Game ending.

The 8 additional red gourds

  • Heavy Golf Ball
    The hole is up on the endgame ridge but the ball is at the red-area golf course, so you have to haul that big, back-breaking ball all the way over. You cannot throw or kick it — only pass it between two players or push the carrier for speed. The route crosses a mountain pass, the train and the drawbridge, so take drops and slopes slowly; if it falls off the chairlift it costs you roughly 10 minutes. This is a separate puzzle from the purple 'Golf · Heavy Object' — that one is the ridge's purple version.
  • Moving Coordinate Box
    A box has to be carried to spots that shift as the on-screen coordinates change, then both players press a button together at the goal. Heavily relies on the Map Room 2D map + GPS: one player reads coordinates and calls the way, the other hauls the box, counting the steps so you do not overshoot. Same 'read the coordinates' idea as 4166 / 1899.
  • Green Flare Puzzle
    One player fires a green flare and another follows the indicated direction to a hidden box by the seaside, with both sides operating at once to finish. This is the same mechanic as the 'green light signal' on your Blue Tower page — the instance you come back for to top up red gourds.
  • Seaside Counting / Binocular
    One player counts the icons flashing across the water and converts them to symbol values, reading the display out to a teammate with binoculars far away, who reads back the answer to fill in. Similar to the 'blue signal headcount' on the Green Tower page; agree on the order you count and which symbol equals which number before you start, so nobody counts from the wrong side.
  • Blue Slope Music Blocks
    First memorise the shapes on the drawing by the hut on the slope, then find the matching music blocks further up and carry them back, slotting each into its place by the drawing. The four shapes look nearly identical, so settle the reading order before you move anything.
  • Dome Microphone
    One player sits in the headset chair with headphones on, while another walks around the dome testing each microphone; when a path comes through clearest, the seated player presses the button to record it, and you aim each one in turn. Similar to the Yellow Tower 'microphone' mechanic but a different scene (this is the dome set).
  • Green Seat / Remote Speaker
    One player sits in a green seat listening to a music cue, while another takes a remote speaker to find the device playing the same melody (often in a far corner), confirms it, then comes back to trigger the matching rig. This is another 'pass the sound position across distance' mechanic.
  • Unused Yellow Puzzle
    The Yellow Tower asks for only 5 rewards but more than 5 puzzles sit around it. After the ending, finish the one you skipped and it usually pays out an extra red gourd — the cheapest way to close your red-gourd gap, so sweep the Yellow Tower first.

Why it won't work

Can I do these before the normal ending?

Most additional puzzles were already there during the main game, just not finished — as long as the tower is open and its reward tray is not full you can come back. Only a few (like the Dome Microphone) pay out inside the true-ending content. Safest is to clear them all on one save after the ending.

I finished them but the white key won't rise.

Red gourds are only part of it. Big Game needs all 8 red + 7 purple, 15 in total, into the green dome. Confirm all 15 slots are full first, then go back to the spawn / tutorial area for the white key rather than waiting around.

Aren't these duplicates of my tower pages?

A few really are — the Green Flare, Seaside Counting, Heavy Golf Ball and Moving Coordinate Box are the same set you saw under different names on the tower pages. They are grouped here so you can knock them out in one pass after the ending: the tower pages explain the core mechanic, this page cleans up what you missed and the final gap.

Can I still play after the true ending?

Yes. After the Big Game completion screen the game still offers Continue, so you can keep exploring the same save, collect achievements or replay challenges.
