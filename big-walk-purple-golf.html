Golf · Heavy Object

The longest and fiddliest one. The hole is on Challenge Ridge, but the ball is at the golf course in the red area, so you have to carry it all the way. You can't throw or kick it — only pass it back and forth.

Before you start

  • Reached Challenge Ridge, chairlift unlocked (5 red gourds)
  • At least 2 players

How to solve it

  1. 01

    Grab the ball in the red area

    Pick up the ball at the golf course in the red area, near the Red Tower.

  2. 02

    Pass it back and forth

    You can't throw or kick it — only pass between two players. Push the carrier to speed up.

  3. 03

    Through the pass to the green section

    Carry the ball along the mountain pass route into the green section.

  4. 04

    Up and right to the rock by the chairlift

    Keep going up and right to the rock point near the chairlift.

  5. 05

    Ride the chairlift east (exit early)

    Take the ball onto the chairlift. Get ready to exit before the station so neither the ball nor a player gets knocked off.

  6. 06

    Purple tunnel to the hole

    After getting off, carry the ball through the purple tunnel, then up and right to sink it into the hole.

Mechanics · Route · Roles · Speed up

The core mechanic

The hole is on Challenge Ridge but the ball is at the red-area golf course, so you carry it all the way over. You can't throw or kick it — only pass it back and forth, or push the carrier for speed. The route often crosses a mountain pass, the train and the drawbridge. It's the main-route 'heavy ball' idea, but this one counts toward a purple gourd.

Roles

At least two players: one carries the ball as the main mover, the other spots ahead, pushes and steers. Carrying is tiring, so swap hands now and then, but do the swap on flat ground — not on a slope or a narrow gap where it can slip.

The route and the risks

Typical route: grab it in the red area → carry the mountain pass into the green section → up and right to the rock by the chairlift → ride the chairlift east (get ready to exit near the stop) → purple tunnel → up and right into the hole. The big risk is the ball falling off the chairlift, which sends it around the whole island for about 10 minutes.

Speed it up

Two players passing relays the ball faster than one person hugging it; slow down on the drawbridge and tight gaps rather than risking a drop over a couple of seconds. On the chairlift don't wait until the station — call 'next stop' before you get there.

Why it won't work

Ball fell off the chairlift?

It loops the whole island, about 10 minutes. Either wait it out or restart.

Can I do it solo?

The ball has to be passed back and forth, so you need at least 2 players.
