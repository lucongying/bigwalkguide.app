Frequently asked questions

Common questions by category. Tap any entry for the answer; see the tower, puzzle, and achievement pages for full walkthroughs.

Game basics · Before you buy

What kind of game is Big Walk?

A 2–12 player co-op puzzle game from House House. Everyone explores an open island together with no combat, health bars, deaths, quest markers, minimap, or tutorial text — where to go and what to do is entirely up to you. It's from the team behind Untitled Goose Game, and it launched on August 4, 2026, as a PlayStation Plus day-one title.

Which company made it?

House House (the Untitled Goose Game studio) developed it; Panic published it. It's House House's first multiplayer co-op game.

When did Big Walk come out?

August 4, 2026, on PC, Mac, PS5, and Nintendo Switch 2 at the same time. It launched into PS Plus for free and quickly sold over a million copies.

What platforms is it on?

PC (Steam and Epic), Mac (Steam or Mac App Store), PS5, and Nintendo Switch 2. There is no Xbox, PS4, or original Switch version.

How does crossplay work?

All four platforms can play together with no extra setup, as long as the version numbers start the same (e.g., both 1.4.x). Cross-platform groups join via a session code; same-platform groups join straight from the friends list.

How much does it cost? Do I need a paid subscription for multiplayer?

Steam lists it at about $19.99, with console versions close. There was a 25% launch discount (roughly $14.99, through August 18), and the PS5 build was free with PS Plus Aug 4–31. For online play: Steam needs no subscription, PS5 needs PlayStation Plus, and Switch 2 needs Nintendo Switch Online — check each store.

Do I need to buy it once per person? Is there a friend pass?

No friend pass and no gifting scheme — everyone needs their own copy on their own platform. Best value is claiming it during the PS Plus free window or a sale.

How many players max, and how do you set the room size?

2–12 players. The host picks the room size at setup: 2-player, 3-player, or 4+. These are three separate world tunings; anything from 4 up to 12 shares the same 'four-plus' layout. Setting the wrong size leaves some puzzles unactivated, and you can't change it mid-run — you have to quit and re-host.

Can I play single-player?

No. There's no solo mode, almost every puzzle needs at least two people, and you'll get hard-stuck alone. There's also no public matchmaking to find you teammates — you invite friends or share a code.

Is there local co-op or split-screen?

No. It's online-only: no local co-op, no split-screen, and no LAN — everyone needs their own device and a copy. House House even advises against playing in the same room, since hearing each other face-to-face removes the distance-based communication the game is built on.

Can I match with strangers?

No. There's no public lobby and no matchmaking — you join via a platform friends list or the host's session code. To find people, check the pinned Big Walk friend-finding thread on Reddit or forums like PC Gamer's.

Is there combat, death, or punishment?

No. There's no combat, health bars, or death anywhere; the difficulty comes purely from puzzle logic and how well you communicate. Stumbling off a cliff just drops the thing you're holding, and it can be recovered.

Is there a minimap, quest markers, or guidance?

None — that's the whole point. No quest markers, no arrows, no tutorial text. You navigate with the paper map, a compass, the GPS, and teammates' descriptions, which is exactly why a guide site like this exists.

How do reviewers rate it? Is it worth buying?

Critically acclaimed — called one of the best new releases of 2026. Metacritic sits around 92–94 (PC 92 / PS5 88 / Switch 2 75), OpenCritic 89 with 91% of critics recommending it, plus Eurogamer 5/5, GameSpot 9/10, and a 'Very Positive' Steam rating. The main caveats: the Switch 2 version has technical issues and scores notably lower, and it's co-op only. If you have a crew, it's worth it — and it's a no-brainer during the PS Plus free window.

What are the PC system requirements?

Minimum: Windows 10 64-bit / Intel i7-7700K or AMD Ryzen 5 1600X / 12 GB RAM / NVIDIA GTX 750 Ti 2GB, AMD R7 370 2GB, or Intel Arc A380 6GB. Recommended: Windows 11 / i5-10600K or Ryzen 7 3700X / 16 GB / GTX 1070 8GB, RX 5600 XT 8GB, or Intel Arc A750 8GB. On Mac you want macOS 15 and Apple Silicon M1 or better (M3+ for smooth). Note the requirements look modest but it is reasonably demanding — a few players report frame drops and stutter.

Is there cross-save or cross-progression?

No. All progress lives on the host's machine and account, and can't be moved to another platform or another player. To continue on a different device you need the same host on the same save.

How long does it take to finish?

The main story is roughly 8–12 hours (faster with a sharp crew, up to ~13 if you explore a lot); 100% with all achievements and the true ending is about 15–20 hours. More players can split up and solve faster, but too many adds coordination overhead.

Is it hard? Is it good for someone new to puzzles?

The entry bar is low but it leans hard on communication. The puzzles themselves aren't tricky — the challenge is getting everyone to clearly describe what they're seeing. Most people don't get stuck because they're not smart enough; they get stuck because no one says it clearly.

Is Big Walk like It Takes Two?

A little, but more open. Both force cooperation, but Big Walk has no combat and no linear story — it's an open-island 2–12 player puzzle game, closer to 'a talking Witness.' It also expects you to use the built-in proximity voice chat rather than each sitting on a headset in Discord.

Getting started · Lobby & co-op

What should I watch for on a first play?

Don't skip the practice area — learn waving, buttons, and the light boxes first. Head out with light and comms gear, because night really is black and normal voice only carries a couple of metres. Three other rookie habits: don't wander off without a word (the map is wide and quiet — drifting out of range costs the team ten minutes of regrouping); don't double up on gear (if two of you hold binoculars, nobody's carrying the whiteboard); and slow down on climbs and ledges instead of rushing.

What non-puzzle setup should we do before starting?

Spend two minutes in the spawn area checking everyone's mic or text input: walk apart, step behind an obstacle, and confirm the voice actually fades and muffles so you catch a dead mic early. Agree on one simple regroup signal (a flare, cowbell, or a named landmark) rather than everyone shouting different things. Then make the steadiest, most available friend the host — that machine owns the save.

What do we do in the tutorial (red bridge)?

Complete four co-op mini-puzzles in any order: in the blue room one player reads a pattern while a teammate replicates it outside; in the green building press all the switches at once; build a human ladder on the yellow scaffolding to reach a high button; and use the binoculars with the box for the last item. Put the four red items in the crown structure by the bridge, run the key through the five cutters, and the bridge lowers.

What do we get for finishing the tutorial?

Cross the bridge to pick up the flashlight, giving you a steady directional light for night exploring. Flashlights mostly sit in the control towers, so don't miss them along the way.

How do I set the room size when hosting?

Match the real player count: 2-player, 3-player, or 4+. Setting it wrong leaves some puzzles unactivated, and it can't be changed mid-run — you have to quit and re-host. For a 'just right' feel, 3 is a common pick: a duo can feel lonely out of earshot, and past six it turns into a party game where solving a single puzzle gets harder.

How do I get friends across platforms into the session? Full steps?

The host picks 'Host Game' from the main menu → new world or load a save → choose the room size (2 / 3 / 4+) → once the character is in the world, find the session code under the pause menu's 'Session details' and send it to friends. They pick 'Join Game': same-platform friends join from the friends list; cross-platform friends enter the code at the top of the screen. Note that entering a code drops you straight into the spawn — make sure everyone is ready first, or the group scatters at the start. The Mac App Store build doesn't support Game Center, so it's code-only there.

How do I keep strangers out?

Set a password when you host. Rooms are visible to friends and friends-of-friends by default, and a password blocks anyone who isn't invited.

How far does voice / text chat reach?

Both are only about 2–3 metres — closer sounds louder and text reads bigger, and terrain, walls, and facing can muffle or cut the audio. Once you're apart you rely on walkie-talkies, radios, or flares.

How do I use the walkie-talkie?

Held in hand it transmits and receives; clipped to your back or hip it only receives. The signal isn't infinite — too far and it degrades from clear to crackly to dead. The low-effort setup is one per squad: the person directing keeps it in hand, and anyone who only needs to listen wears it.

How do I use the radio?

A fixed station — hold the red key by the mic to talk, and your voice carries to every receiver point on the map. Anyone standing next to a receiver can hear you without pressing anything. Receiver points are mostly on tower rooftops, the map room, the last train car, and the central peak.

What are the megaphone and flare for?

There are only a few megaphones on the map and they carry further than normal voice, great for shouting down from a mountain or tower. Flares sit at towers and high spots — fire one when separated so teammates can see where you are.

What do I do when it's too dark at night?

Carry a flashlight or lantern, or grab one of the big red-orange glowing orbs to move the light with you. Smarter: roll a glowing orb to a junction before nightfall as a landmark, so a split group knows where to regroup. Worst case, just wait for sunrise.

Can I skip or speed up the day/night cycle?

No. The cycle can't be turned off or accelerated, and night's darkness is deliberate — you either push through with light sources or wait for dawn.

Where do backpacks and fanny packs come from, and how many slots?

Look in the round blue buildings — 9 blue buildings across the map, 10 packs total. A backpack holds almost anything; a fanny pack only takes small items like the map. Both need a teammate to equip them for you. Backpack + fanny pack + one in hand = 3 items max, and anything stowed in a pack survives a cliff fall.

I dropped something off a cliff — can I get it back?

Don't panic. Anything you're still holding when the host ends the session appears on the Lost & Found table at spawn next time; anything you weren't holding returns to its original spawn point.

What are the default PC controls?

WASD to move, Space to jump (chained jumps and jump-uphill move faster); Q raises the left arm, E the right, Q+E both; left click picks up / uses (also toggles a lantern or lifts a teammate to stack), right click drops / throws; Esc opens the menu. Keybinds are rebindable in settings — some remap jump to a scroll wheel, grab to F, and throw to G for the ball-climb trick.

Where is the save? Can another player become host?

All progress lives in the host's save. If that host isn't around, you can't continue. House House documents no way to transfer the save to another player, so it's best to keep the same person hosting.

How do I find people to play with?

Post in the pinned Big Walk friend-finding thread on Reddit or on community forums like PC Gamer's — plenty of people are looking for a group.

How do I wave / signal teammates?

Raising your arm is the most basic gesture and the tutorial teaches it; Q and E control the left and right hands. Once you're apart, fall back on gestures, flares, and the walkie-talkie.

Main route · The five towers

Which tower should I do first?

The Red Tower. It unlocks the Map Room, and once you've grabbed the paper map and compass, finding your way everywhere gets a lot easier.

What's the order of the five towers?

Red (Map Room) → Green (chairlift) → Yellow (tunnels) → Blue (monorail) → Black (normal ending). The open world doesn't lock the order, but this route is the smoothest.

What's the common flow for each tower?

Each is basically the same: collect the coloured gourds, feed them to the key dispenser on the rooftop, shape the blank key on the cutters, then deliver the finished key to unlock the relevant system.

Why do I have to cut the key first?

This is the number-one reason people get stuck. The key you receive is a blank — it won't fit any lock until you run it through the cutters to shape it into the target form.

How do I clear the Red Tower and open the Map Room?

The Red Tower has five puzzles (green room pattern, yellow maze timer, train cannon, basketball court, hillside gestures). Collect the five red gourds, put them in the rooftop console to get a key, run it through the cutters, then use it to open the Map Room near spawn, which holds the paper map and compass.

What's the difference between the map-room maps?

One is a room-sized 3D terrain map where flags mark what's done — a standing flag without a rod means not yet solved, a flat one means collected but not submitted. The other is a foldable portable map that zooms and labels regions — great for reading coordinates, but it never shows your live position, so you still need the GPS to pin yourself.

What does the Green Tower unlock? How do I activate the chairlift?

It unlocks the chairlift. Finish the Green Tower's puzzles and cut the gold key, then insert it at the station (about 3950, 1950) to ride it. Riding also earns Big Sit.

How do I get through the Green Tower's soundproof glass room?

Press the two diagonal buttons to lower the glass and trap somebody; find the hidden button behind a wooden beam to reveal a symbol plate. The trapped player relays the symbol order to the outside teammate (E+Q to display a symbol, nod to confirm), the outside player enters that order and presses the button under the black panel, and the glass drops.

What does the Yellow Tower unlock? How do I enter the tunnels?

It unlocks the underground tunnel network — the fastest way across the island. After the Yellow Tower, entries are scattered around the map, and the No.5 radio nightclub is hidden in that tunnel network.

What does the Blue Tower unlock? How do I ride the train?

It unlocks the monorail. You have to actually ride a stretch for it to count; just unlocking the system isn't enough. The train won't stop on its own — someone has to be on board holding the brake.

What's the Black Tower? Where's the black sphere?

The final tower, leading to the normal ending (Big Goodbye). Gather enough black gourds from the first four towers to forge the Black Tower key, open the great wall gate to the west, and deep inside lies the giant black sphere — finish its final puzzle to trigger the normal ending.

How do I get past the great wall — can I climb it early?

The proper route: clear the towers (especially the completed Black Tower key), return to the great wall gate in the west, and in the secret room between the broken black-sphere pieces use the key to open the gate, then walk out the far side for Big Wall. Climbing it early is a workaround: remap jump to the mouse scroll wheel, put grab/throw on adjacent keys (F and G work well), carry a pink ball into a Wall corner, angle your back into the corner holding S, and rapidly alternate grab/drop while scrolling up so the ball's physics lifts you. It doesn't count as a proper Big Wall, it's fiddly, and it's not worth relying on.

How do I read map coordinates?

Treat the two-digit grid as ending in 00: first go left/right for the first number, then up/down for the second; the crossing is your target. For example 4166 sits between 41 (4100) and 42 (4200), and 1899 is just below the 19 (1900) line.

I'm lost — what do I do?

Open the Map Room for the paper map plus compass, or grab the GPS near the Green Tower/chairlift to read live coordinates; worst case, fire a flare so a teammate can see where you are.

Can I do the towers out of order?

Yes, the open world doesn't lock the order, but Red first and then Green → Yellow → Blue gives the smoothest experience.

How do I trigger the normal ending?

Complete the Black Tower, pass through the wall gate, enter the giant black sphere and finish its final puzzle, then walk to the ending building, wave and press the button, and watch the first credit sequence — that's the normal ending and unlocks Big Goodbye.

What does the Black Tower require?

Clear the first four towers and gather enough black gourds to forge the Black Tower key, which opens the great wall gate in the west.

What are the GPS and compass for?

The GPS shows your live coordinates so you can pin a target quickly; the compass pairs with the paper map for direction, and is especially useful when travelling at night.

Puzzle solutions · Stuck?

What does 4166 / 1899 mean?

It's a map coordinate, not a password. 4166 lands roughly between the 41 and 42 lines and 1899 just below the 19 line; the crossing sits mid-south of the island among a rock pile between the rail bend and where the chairlift dips into the tunnel. A button disguised as a rock hides there, wired to the locked chest on the beach.

How do I solve 4166 / 1899?

Get the paper map or GPS first. Leave one player by the beach chest and send another to the coordinate to find and hold the disguised button on the rock face. While it's held the chest opens, and the guard grabs the red gourd inside — coordinate over the walkie or a flare.

Can one person solve 4166 / 1899?

No. The button and chest are far apart and you need someone at each end. The chest closes the moment the button is released, so the guard must take the item before the holder lets go.

Why does the chest keep closing?

It only stays open while the button is held — don't release before your teammate takes the item.

How does key-cutting actually work?

Put the blank key on the first cutter, which shows an arrow to the next. Follow the arrows across all five cutters until it's shaped into the target form, then it fits the lock. Miss or miscut a stage and it won't go in.

What puzzles are in the Red Tower?

Five: the green room (replicate a symbol from inside and out), the yellow maze (carry a timed bomb to a green can), the train cannon (fire a timer across for a teammate to catch), the basketball court (toss light bulbs into the hoop to light them all), and the hillside gestures (one poses while the other picks a card through binoculars).

What puzzles are in the Green Tower?

Centred on the chairlift, with the red soundproof glass room at its core (see the main route for the solution). Completing it powers the chairlift, and riding it earns Big Sit.

What puzzles are in the Yellow Tower?

Built around the tunnels; finishing unlocks the underground shortcut, and the No.5 radio nightclub hides in that network.

What puzzles are in the Blue Tower?

Built around the monorail; finishing unlocks the train, and riding a stretch earns Big Ride.

What are the purple puzzles?

Seven hard post-game puzzles unlocked after the normal ending — tougher co-op variants of earlier puzzles. All seven must be done for the true ending.

Where do I find the purple puzzles?

They only activate after the normal ending. Take the chairlift to Challenge Ridge — some sit behind purple gates and others are scattered around the island.

What are the seven purple puzzles?

The 30-minute timer, the weight/golf, the wall of light, the music box, the shape pairing, the number code, and the purple flag. See the purple-puzzle pages for each solution.

How do I solve the purple flag golf?

The ball isn't at the purple flag. Go back to the original golf puzzle in the hills, grab a ball, and carry it across the map (usually via the train station, Green Tower, and rope bridges) to the purple flag. Don't drop it on the bridges — you'll just have to fetch another.

How do I solve the weight / golf (heavy ball)?

Two players pass a heavy ball hand-to-hand: one picks it up, the other takes it from them, and you alternate carrying it up to the red flag slot on the slope. This is pure coordination — keep calling who's receiving and who's passing.

How do I solve the invisible symbols / lens puzzle?

In the round blue building, one player looks through a fixed lens while the other holds each of the nine blank plates up to the platform, revealing a hidden symbol on each. Arrange the plates in a 3×3 grid, close the building door to show a five-symbol sequence, then place the plates in that order.

I'm stuck — what should I check?

Confirm the room size is right, then check you haven't missed a prerequisite (key not cut, map not taken, gourds not fed), and finally go over the fine details with your teammate.

Why won't a puzzle activate?

Most often the room size is wrong. Some puzzles need a minimum player count and stay dormant otherwise — quit and re-host with the correct size.

Can two players clear every puzzle?

Most puzzles work with two, but some are tuned for four, so extra players make them more forgiving, and a few genuinely need another set of hands.

How do I solve a number-code puzzle?

These map a pattern or sequence to numbers, so quiz your teammates on the details (filled or outlined, how many dots, which way it faces) rather than assuming.

Collectibles & coordinates

What kinds of collectibles are there?

Coordinate-based collectibles, backpacks/fanny packs, seven music radio channels, plus the eight red and seven purple gourds used for the endings.

How many backpacks are there, and where?

Ten across nine blue buildings — the one in the southeast holds both a backpack and a fanny pack. Known spots include the yellow pack (1380, 3750), green pack (1660, 3720), light-blue pack (1540, 3500), pink pack (1775, 4275), and red pack (4340, 2025). Open the map and follow the blue circular icons for the fastest route.

What's the difference between a fanny pack and a backpack?

A backpack holds almost anything, a fanny pack only small items like the map, and neither takes a round lantern. Both need a teammate to equip them for you — a white ring lights up on the helper while they do it.

How many radio channels are there, and how do I unlock them?

Seven music channels. Find the green transmitter box at each spot and press the red button to unlock its song, then switch channels on the handheld radio. They aren't numbered in order — the usual sequence is red, light green, yellow, blue, pink, purple, then the No.5 nightclub channel last.

Where are the radios? Give me a few coords.

No.1 red (~3700, 1190) near spawn, No.2 purple (~4255, 2010) far south, No.3 light green (~3840, 1640) near the Red Tower, No.4 yellow (~3313, 1837) downhill from the Blue Tower, No.5 inside the Yellow Tower's tunnel nightclub, No.6 blue (~3930, 1800) northwest of the Green Tower, and No.7 pink (~3310, 1275) west of the Yellow Tower.

What are the red and purple gourds?

Progression items for the endings: the normal ending needs eight red gourds, and the true ending needs a further seven purple gourds — 15 in total.

How does the coordinate system work?

The map edges carry a coordinate grid. Read the target coordinates, line them up against the paper map or GPS, and the crossing is your destination. Treat each two-digit grid number as ending in 00.

Where do I get the GPS?

Near the Green Tower / chairlift. Once you have it, it shows your live coordinates so you can lock onto a target quickly.

If I miss a collectible, can I go back?

Yes. It's an open world, so you can go back anytime. But you must finish the collection before the true ending — missing one means no white key.

Do collectibles affect achievements?

Yes. Achievements like Big Pack (backpack) and Big Help (fanny pack) depend on them, and a full collection is also a prerequisite for the true ending.

Where do I submit the 15 gourds?

At the green dome near the Map Room / tutorial area — it has 15 slots for the eight red plus seven purple gourds. Filling it raises the white key.

Achievements & endings

How many achievements are there?

Steam has 12; PS5 adds a platinum trophy for 13. Switch 2 has no corresponding trophy system.

Which achievements are easy to miss?

Big Climb (summit), Big Makeover (get shiny), and Big Game (true ending) are the three easiest to miss; Big Pack (backpack) and Big Help (fanny pack) also need deliberate equipping. The rest mostly come along the main route.

How do I get Big Climb?

Ride the Green Tower chairlift to the highest stop, then hike up to the red-symbol peak at the summit — every player must reach the top themselves. Riding to the top doesn't auto-unlock it.

How do I get Big Makeover?

Clear the Yellow Tower to open the tunnels, find the hidden second barber/makeover station in the network, and give yourself a shiny gold, silver, or bronze finish.

How do I do the normal ending?

Clear the five towers, wrap up the Black Tower, enter the black sphere and finish its final puzzle, then wave and press the button through the credits to unlock Big Goodbye.

How do I do the true ending?

Reach the normal ending first, then continue in the same save: finish every regular puzzle plus the seven purple puzzles, collect all 15 gourds, fill the green dome for the white key, and take it back to the cracked black sphere at spawn.

Where is the white (master) key?

Once your world is truly 100% complete, the white key rises from the ground near spawn. If it hasn't appeared, something is still unsolved.

The white key won't show up — what do I do?

That means a puzzle is unfinished, most often one regular puzzle or one of the seven purple ones. Check the 3D terrain map in the Map Room — an empty flag-pole marks what's left.

Where do I use the white key?

Return to the tutorial spawn, find the cracked black sphere on the wall, insert the white key, walk through the hidden room, and pass through the bright white opening for the true ending.

How do I get the PS5 platinum?

Earn the other 12 trophies (including the Big Game true ending) to auto-unlock the platinum Big Trophy.

What are the hidden achievements?

Big Goodbye (normal ending) and Big Game (true ending) are the two hidden ones, unlocked by finishing their respective endings.

What's the difference between the master key and a tower key?

Tower keys are temporary and spent once used; the master (white) key is the ultimate reward for 100% and opens the true-ending hidden door.

What's the difference between the true and normal endings?

The normal ending only needs the five towers; the true ending requires 100% in the same save — every regular puzzle, the seven purple puzzles, and all 15 gourds — for a fuller finale.

Do riding the chairlift, train, or entering tunnels also pop achievements?

Yes. Big Sit (chairlift), Big Ride (train), and Big Tunnel (tunnels) match main-route unlocks, but you have to actually use them once — just unlocking the system doesn't count.

Troubleshooting · Errors

A teammate's voice is too quiet?

Update to 1.4.8 (PC) / 1.005 (console) — the patch improved volume calibration for quiet microphones. A muted mic can also take a few seconds to calibrate, so talk first and let it auto-adjust.

Voice is still unclear after the update?

Check Settings → Audio for whether the microphone-activity bar reacts, confirm the mic isn't muted and the mute mode isn't 'push-to-talk'; it can also be network, so have the host use ethernet where possible.

Can I make the voice range longer?

No. House House has confirmed it won't change the voice-falloff distance — it's the core design. For range, use walkies, radios, or flares.

Crossplay won't connect?

First confirm the first two version numbers match (e.g., both 1.4.x) — the last digit doesn't matter. Then make sure the host is loaded into the world, and grab the current session's code from the pause-menu 'Session details' to send to friends rather than an old one. Same-platform players should just use the friends list.

My connection keeps dropping?

Check the host's network first — lower the load or switch to wired; choppy audio is usually a network problem.

My progress / save is gone?

Progress lives in the host's save. Confirm it's still the same host and the same save; re-hosting with a different host starts a fresh world.

Does everyone disconnect when the host leaves?

Yes — when the host ends the session, everyone disconnects. So keep the host online and save important progress regularly.

The screen is too dark / can I adjust brightness?

You can adjust brightness in settings, but night's darkness is deliberate — lean on light sources rather than cranking the slider.

Where are the overall audio settings?

Settings → Audio controls the volume and shows microphone-activity feedback; that's where you start for any voice issue.

Can I rebind the controls?

Yes, rebind in settings — the exact keys depend on the platform. PC defaults are listed under Getting Started.

Does it have official Chinese?

Check the in-game language setting; most builds ship with multiple languages, so look at the store page for details.

I'm stuck on a bug — what do I do?

First check whether a prerequisite is unfinished (key not cut, puzzle not activated); if it's a real bug, look for a patch or have the host reload the session.
