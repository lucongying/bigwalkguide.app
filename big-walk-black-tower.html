Unlock the Black Tower and reach the normal ending

The Black Tower is the final tower and the last gate between your group and the ending. The first time you walk up, it hypnotizes one player, plays a short cutscene, and shows a key on the roof you can't touch yet — that's the goal you're working toward. It's more of a lock than a maze of rooms: feed it 6 puzzle collectibles, take the roof key, open the Wall, then finish the Black Sphere sequence to trigger the normal Big Goodbye ending. Afterward, continuing the same save opens the seven purple puzzles on the true-ending route.

Before you start

  • Finished all four colored towers (Red, Green, Yellow, Blue) — only then does the Black Tower door open
  • At least 2 players — every puzzle needs coordination
  • Three voice-disabled puzzles — agree on gesture/light/jump signals before you start
  • The Black Tower needs 6 collectibles (not 5); purple and red are interchangeable, so six normal ones are enough

How to solve it

  1. 01

    Finish the four colored towers — the Black Tower opens

    The Black Tower (Black Obelisk) can't be forced open early, no matter how many collectibles you're carrying. Its door unlocks on its own once Red, Green, Yellow, and Blue are all complete.

  2. 02

    Watch the cutscene — the tower hypnotizes one player

    The first time you approach, the tower triggers a short cutscene: it hypnotizes one player and shows the roof key you can't touch yet. Remember it — you'll need six collectibles to bring it down.

  3. 03

    Solve the 6 Black Tower puzzles and bank 6 gourds

    Solving a puzzle isn't the same as claiming its reward. Each one drops a gourd-shaped collectible that's easy to leave behind, so count that you're carrying six before returning. Purple and red slot into the same sockets and are interchangeable, so six normal ones are enough. If your group is truly stuck on one, the host can enable challenge skipping from the accessibility menu so no puzzle blocks the finish. See the list below.

  4. 04

    Place all six and climb for the roof key

    Bring all six back to the Black Tower and slot them in one by one. Once the sixth is in, the final progression opens and you can climb to the roof for the key you saw in the cutscene. Note: progression trophies unlock when you personally cross, ride, or interact — not when the group does it — so do the tower interactions together.

  5. 05

    Take the Black Key to the Wall and open it

    The Black Key unlocks the large barred gate on the far western side of the island. Walk through to pop the Big Wall trophy. Beyond it is a long, bare stretch with the Black Sphere waiting on the horizon and no second key to build along the way — head straight for it.

  6. 06

    Enter the Black Sphere, clear the rooms, press the final button

    The Black Sphere is the final area of the normal route. Work through its cooperative rooms until you reach a dark red chamber with a suspended mechanism and a button nearby. Press the button, then don't rush back to the main island — wait for the mechanism to power down and the sphere to open, and only then does the ending building appear across the water.

  7. 07

    Follow the red route across the water, wave goodbye, and enter

    Drop down from the opened sphere and follow the visible red route out over the water to the small ending building. At the stairs, stop at the sign and use the Wave Goodbye gesture (the keyboard prompt shows Q or E), then press the entrance button — these are two separate interactions, and you need both. Enter with your group and finish the short red-lit room to trigger the normal Big Goodbye ending.

The 6 Black Tower puzzles (3 voice-disabled + 3 action)

  • Blue Locked Room SymbolsNo voice
    Down and left of the Black Tower entrance is a locked room: a row of switches outside, a meter above them, one player inside and one at the switches. Seven symbols light up at random inside and the inside player can't speak. Number the seven symbols before you begin — 1, 2, 3 across the top and 4, 5, 6, 7 for the ones on the buttons. When a symbol lights up, signal its number with a countable gesture (e.g. jump six times for symbol six). The outside player presses the matching switch; the meter only rises on a correct press, so patience beats speed, and it drops the collectible when full.
  • Green Room Red LightNo voice
    From the tower entrance head right and drop down near the two large green-grey pillars to find two rooms: one shows a tile pattern, the other is where you rebuild it, and voice is disabled. The rebuilding player places a tile in front of a one-way blackboard, and the player who can see the pattern reads the tile's position through it and answers with a red light. Agree on the code first — the number of flashes means the slot number, and a burst of rapid flashes means a tile doesn't belong in the pattern at all. Place each tile, confirm it, and repeat until both sides match.
  • Double Green BarnNo voice
    The barn sits near the Green Wall; from the Yellow Tower head straight right to reach it, with the collectible waiting out front. Inside, one room shows a sequence of physical poses that your teammate has to recreate with tiles, again without voice. One player studies the pose sequence, then moves to the narrow chamber on the right and acts each gesture out slowly with the character's body. The teammate watches, matches each pose to the right tile, and enters the exact order. Order matters, so if one gesture is missed, replay just that one instead of restarting the run; finishing drops the gourd at the front.
  • Golden Mask Obstacle CourseBlind guide
    One player wears a golden mask that fully blocks their view while the other guides them through an obstacle course. Voice is allowed here, so treat it like steering a remote-control car: push the player through the starting gate and call out one short instruction before each obstacle — when to move, jump, climb, or turn. Red walls can block your line of sight, so keep moving around them. Give one instruction at a time and let them react; at the end the masked player presses the button to finish and hand over the collectible.
  • Chairlift Shape SequenceMemory
    The simplest of the six. One side of the chairlift shows a completed shape sequence, and the other side has loose shapes you carry across and arrange the same way. Memorize the pattern first, then ferry each shape over and place it in both the matching shape and the exact spot. A backpack lets you carry two shapes at once, which cuts the number of chairlift trips in half.
  • Deep WellToss & catch
    A tall vertical room with the collectible at the very bottom. The catch is that grabbing it seals the stairs, so you can't walk back up — you have to throw it from platform to platform. Go to the bottom and pick up the collectible (which closes the door to the stairs), stand close to the edge and throw it up to the first platform while a teammate waits there to catch it before it falls back. Repeat up each level, aiming carefully on the higher gaps, and place it in the box at the top to finish.

Inside the Black Sphere: the co-op gauntlet

First, the room count varies by source

The inside of the sphere is a chain of co-op rooms with no tower name. Guides slice it into 5 rooms (Keengamer) or 7 (VGTimes); it's the same stretch at a different granularity. Know this and you won't panic that 'my third room doesn't match the guide' — just keep clearing co-op rooms until the dark-red room and press the button, and the sphere opens.

Symbol Card Relay

Same idea as the Black Tower type where one player calls symbols and another reproduces them. Scan the symbol set and number it, agree the call-out, then start; follow the in-room prompts for the exact interaction.

Blue Marker Relay / Blue Panels

A set of patterns on blue markers or blue panels you reproduce one at a time, usually a 'look at one side, recreate on the other' matching puzzle. Again, settle the numbering first.

Yellow Control & Door

A set of doors / gates operated by yellow switches, with players operating each side. These control doors appear a few times inside — the rule is keep both sides in sync and never let one side release early.

Red Kettle

Commonly cited as Red Kettle, sometimes filed under 'red kettle'. A room built around red kettle / device mechanisms with one or two switches beside them; follow the exact sequence the room prompts.

Timed Puck & Separate Corridors (some guides)

The 7-room version (VGTimes) adds two types over Keengamer's 5: a timed puck (timing — press the button at the right moment) and separate corridors (two or three players each take a different path and coordinate on the gates). Treat them as a 'timed co-op' and a 'split-path' room respectively.

Colored Magnetic Shapes

Commonly cited as Colored Magnetic Shapes / Image-Matching / Colors & Shapes. Use colored magnetic pieces (or similar blocks) to build a fixed shape per the wall diagram, typically two players: one reads the diagram, one builds, aligning color and position. Matching-and-comparing is the theme of the sphere's later rooms.

Why it won't work

The Black Tower door won't open?

It only opens after all four colored towers (Red, Green, Yellow, Blue) are done. Check you haven't missed one — carrying extra collectibles can't force it open early.

The Black Tower won't give a key?

The usual causes are: ① the four colored towers aren't all done, so the door never opened; ② you solved a puzzle but never picked up its collectible, so you have fewer than six; ③ not all six are placed in the tower's sockets; or ④ you hunted purple collectibles first — those are post-game and not needed here. Count how many you're actually holding.

Stuck inside the Black Sphere?

Voice is restricted inside, so agree on gesture/light signals before entering. This is especially true of the voice-disabled puzzles — a wrong input usually resets your progress.

You waved goodbye but the ending didn't continue?

Wave Goodbye and the entrance button are two separate steps. If you waved but nothing continued, go back to the entrance button and press it before retracing the route. On keyboard the wave prompt is Q or E.

Want the true ending after Big Goodbye?

The normal ending isn't the end. Big Goodbye unlocks seven purple puzzles and leaves the Black Sphere broken open. To chase the Big Game true ending, stay on the same save, finish anything you skipped, and see the true-ending page.
