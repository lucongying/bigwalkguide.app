07FAQ

Stuck? Start with the most-asked questions

Key cutting, reading coordinates, tower order, the true ending — a few of the most common, tap to answer.

Why won't my key open the lock?

The key has to be shaped at a cutter before it opens anything — keys from puzzles can't be used directly. Follow the arrows through all five cutters; it's the #1 reason players get stuck. See the Key Cutting page.

What are 4166 / 1899?

A coordinate, not a code. On the paper map read the first number left/right and the second top/bottom; the crossing is your target. Two players work each end to grab the reward. See the 4166/1899 page.

No minimap — where am I?

The game deliberately gives no quest markers or minimap. Grab the paper map and compass from the Red Tower's Map Room, or a GPS near the Green Tower to see your live coordinates. See the Map & Coordinates page.

How many players? Can I play solo?

2–12 on co-op. There's no solo mode and no public matchmaking — you bring your own group or use a join code. See the Beginner's Guide page.

How do I trigger the true ending?

Clear the normal ending first, then stay on the same save: finish every normal puzzle and the 7 purple ones, deliver 8 red + 7 purple = 15 gourds to the green dome, and the white key rises at the start. See the True Ending page.

Which tower first? What order?

Red → Green → Yellow → Blue → Black. Do the Red Tower first to unlock the Map Room and grab the paper map and compass — finding your way gets much easier after that. The world doesn't lock the order, but this path is smoothest. See the Main Walkthrough page.

Do more players make the puzzles harder?

No — just a few extra switches or a higher target count. The game only scales for 2 / 3 / 4+ players; the solving logic and steps are identical, you just have more hands. See the All Puzzles page.

A key fell off a cliff / went missing?

Uneven terrain makes keys slide further. Stop, confirm who's holding the key and where it landed, then head below to fetch it; items in hand when the host ends the session return to the start point's lost-and-found table. See the Beginner's Guide page.

Too dark to see at night — what do I do?

Carry a flashlight or lantern, or grab one of the glowing red-orange orbs as a moving light. Practical trick: drop orbs at forks before dark as markers so you can regroup after splitting up. The day/night cycle can't be skipped, so you just power through with light.

Can Steam / PS5 / Switch play together?

Yes — crossplay is on. All four platforms connect with no extra setup, just needing matching major versions; cross-platform rooms use the join code, same-platform uses friends. There's no Xbox or PS4 version. See the Beginner's Guide page.
