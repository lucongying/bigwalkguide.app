Every collectible: 10 bags + 7 radio stations

Big Walk's collectibles come in two kinds: 10 bags (6 backpacks + 4 hip bags, tucked inside blue circular structures) and 7 radio station channels. Bags are not just cosmetic — they buy you up to two extra carry slots for key puzzle items and comms gear, and collecting them drives the Big Pack and Big Help achievements. Each radio station is a green antenna transmitter you activate to add songs to your handheld radio. All coordinates below.

The 10 bags (6 backpacks + 4 hip bags)

  • Yellow Backpack1380, 3750
    Southwest of the Red Tower. The first bag most players grab — head that way after the red drawbridge; it hangs on the central pole inside the blue circular 'garage' structure.
  • Green Backpack1660, 3720
    On the central mountain slope, north of the light-green radio music area. Walk up the slope from the music zone to the blue circular structure.
  • Pink Backpack1775, 4275
    Blue structure west of the southern chairlift station. Ride the chairlift south, then head a bit west.
  • Blue Backpack1540, 3500
    Northeast of the Map Room. After opening the Red Tower map room, take a short route northeast and look for the blue circular building.
  • Pastel Red Backpack3310, 2000
    By the beach far east of the Yellow Tower, toward the green barrier. Look for the blue circular building on the beach.
  • Red Backpack4340, 2025
    Southeast corner; ride the chairlift all the way down, then walk southeast. This structure also holds the blue hip bag — grab two at once.
  • Yellow Hip Bag1860, 3750
    On the mountainside northwest of the Green Tower. The triangular hip bag hangs inside a blue circular structure near the Green Tower.
  • Green Hip Bag1330, 3440
    Northwest of the Map Room / southwest of the Yellow Tower. There is a blue circular structure on this slope.
  • Red Hip Bag3220, 1720
    Northeast of the Yellow Tower. Move a little northeast from the Yellow Tower puzzle area into the blue circular building.
  • Blue Hip Bag4340, 2025
    Southeast corner — the same blue structure as the Red Backpack. Grab the backpack first, then hang the hip bag.

Backpacks & hip bags: how to use them

You can only hold 1 item by default

Without a bag each player can only hold one thing in their hands. A backpack adds one slot on your back, a hip bag adds one on your waist — giving up to 3 carrying slots including what is in your hands. If you want to haul puzzle parts, keys or the map, expanding your carry space is the most practical upgrade.

You cannot equip it yourself — a teammate must

Neither backpacks nor hip bags can be put on by yourself. A teammate picks the bag up, walks over (a white circle lights up on your avatar while they hold it), and uses an interact button to strap it onto your back or hip. Taking it off, and putting items in or taking them out, are all team actions — you can only hold something in your own hands.

Items stored in a bag survive a fall

Something gripped in your hands will drop if you fall off a cliff or tumble, but anything stowed in a backpack or hip bag stays put. That is why the map, keys and gourds should be carried in a bag rather than held loose.

Backpack vs hip bag: what each can carry

A backpack fits nearly anything, including large puzzle pieces; a hip bag only fits small items — walkie-talkies and handheld radios are perfect for it, so treat it as your 'comms pocket'. Neither bag holds the round lanterns; those must be set down or carried by hand.

Spot them: blue circular 'wastepaper basket' buildings

Every bag hangs inside a blue, circular building that looks like a small parking garage or a wastepaper basket, with a short blue ramp at the entrance. There are 9 such structures and each holds one bag — except the far-southeast one, which holds both a backpack and a hip bag. If you play in a small group, visit several so everyone can expand; equipping one for the first time unlocks Big Pack, and a hip bag unlocks Big Help.

The hidden purple hip bag sources record (optional)

Most sources count 10 bags (6 backpacks + 4 hip bags); Screen Hype and others also document a purple hip bag hidden in the tunnels — enter the Yellow Tunnel and, before the giant red staircase leading to the Shiny customisation room, take the small blue entrance on the left, or pass it naturally when travelling Red→Green / Green→Red tunnels. It needs the Yellow Tunnel unlocked and is the last, hidden piece of a full collection.

The 7 radio station channels

  • Channel #1 · Red3700, 1190
    Start area, behind the Red Tower at the end of the crosswalk, overlooking the sea. No unlock needed — the first station most players meet, and where you pick up the handheld radio itself.
  • Channel #2 · Purple4250, 2020
    Southern mountain, above the waterside music area near the cable car descent point. Requires the Green Tower to unlock the rope bridge / chairlift route.
  • Channel #3 · Light green3820, 1620
    Forest near the Red Tower puzzle area, at the end of the small path down from the heavy-ball puzzle, behind the music-area speakers. No unlock needed.
  • Channel #4 · Yellow3310, 1850
    After descending the Blue Tower (also called the Blue Lighthouse), down the mountainside into the yellow speaker area by the wall with a window hole. No unlock needed.
  • Channel #5 · SecretTunnel (3500, 1620)
    Neon nightclub DJ booth inside the Yellow Tunnel, with interactive lights. Requires the Yellow Tower tunnel key — take the first right in the tunnel instead of the Red Tower main route.
  • Channel #6 · Blue3990, 1800
    Northwest of the Green Tower, on a pale-blue platform among rocks and trees. Easiest to spot during the day; the one with a staircase is against the rock wall.
  • Channel #7 · Pink3310, 1280
    West and below the Yellow Tower, beside the flat red/pink grid structure near the map edge. Jump down toward the pink building and the green box is just outside it.

How to unlock the radio stations

Look for the green transmitter box, not the handheld radio

Each station is a green antenna transmitter box, usually tucked beside a louder, brighter music landmark (speakers, colourful platforms). Interact with it once and that channel is added to your handheld radio. The radio you actually listen on is the one you carry on you.

The Field Radio at the crosswalk is not a transmitter

The Field Radio by the opening crosswalk is different — it only plays channels you have already unlocked and is not one of the green boxes you are hunting. It is also where you get the portable handheld radio. Treating that station as a collectible point is an easy run for nothing.

Recommended order: 1 → 3 → 4 → 6 → 7 → 2 → 5

The first four unlock as you go: 1 in the start area, 3 by the Red Tower forest, 4 below the Blue Tower and 6 northwest of the Green Tower require nothing. Leave 7 (west of the Yellow Tower), 2 (southern peak — needs the Green Tower rope bridge / chairlift) and 5 (tunnel nightclub — needs the Yellow Tower key) for later. The channel numbers are not a discovery order, so you can do them out of sequence too.

The easiest to miss, #5: the Yellow Tunnel nightclub

Inside the Yellow Tunnel most players follow the main route toward the Red Tower and walk past the turn. To get #5, turn right at the first fork instead of the main route, hug the wall through the darker underground stretch, climb the stairs, and you reach the neon nightclub with lights and dance-floor music — the green box is by the DJ area.

Coordinates shift with the map angle, don't memorise numbers

Coordinates given by different sources can drift a little depending on the map angle. The reliable way to navigate is by the named towers, the speakers and coloured music landmarks, and the colour of the structure beside each transmitter; treat coordinates as a helper and landmarks as your checklist.

Why it won't work

What do backpacks / hip bags do?

They expand carry space: you can hold only 1 item by default, a backpack adds one slot and a hip bag adds another, so up to 3 items, and anything stored inside survives a fall. They are also the requirement for Big Pack (backpack) and Big Help (hip bag), and count toward full completion.

One building holds two?

Yes — the far-southeast blue structure at (4340, 2025) holds both the Red Backpack and the Blue Hip Bag, so you can grab both in one trip.

Can't reach radio #2?

#2 is near the southernmost peak — finish the Green Tower to unlock the rope bridge / chairlift route first. Do not try to climb it before Green Tower is open.

Where is radio #5?

#5 is the secret channel, at the DJ booth in the Yellow Tunnel nightclub. Finish the Yellow Tower to get the tunnel key, then take the first right in the tunnel instead of the Red Tower main route, hugging the wall to the nightclub.

Why can't I put on a backpack / hip bag?

You cannot equip either yourself. Have a teammate pick the bag up and walk to you (a white circle appears on your avatar), then use the interact button to attach it. Removing it and stowing items are also team actions.

Why didn't Big Pack / Big Help pop?

Most likely nobody actually strapped the bag onto you. You cannot do it yourself — a teammate must interact; the moment it is truly equipped the achievement pops, with no other condition.

I unlocked a station but it's not on my handheld radio?

You probably found the Field Radio by the opening crosswalk, which only plays already-unlocked channels and is not one of the game's transmitters. Go into the world and find each green antenna box, then interact with it once to add the channel.

Some sources say there are 11 bags?

Most sources count 10 (6 backpacks + 4 hip bags); Screen Hype and others also count the hidden purple hip bag in the tunnels, making it 11. If you want a full collection you can grab it too — it needs the Yellow Tunnel unlocked (see the mechanics section above).

Can a bag hold a lantern?

No. Round lanterns cannot go into a backpack or hip bag, only carried by hand. To walk the night with light, hold it in your hands or set it down first.
