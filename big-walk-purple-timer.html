Wait 30 Minutes House

The easiest of the 7 purple puzzles. Enter the house and press the yellow button to start the timer. You don't have to stay — go do other challenges and the timer finishes on its own. Come back to collect the purple gourd.

Before you start

  • Reached Challenge Ridge (chairlift + purple tunnel after the normal ending)
  • No items required

How to solve it

  1. 01

    Find the timer house

    Find the small house on Challenge Ridge with a yellow button inside.

  2. 02

    Press the yellow button

    Press it to start the 30-minute countdown immediately.

  3. 03

    Go do other challenges

    You don't have to stay — the timer keeps running. Use the 30 minutes to knock out the other puzzles.

  4. 04

    Return for the gourd

    When the timer hits zero the puzzle auto-completes. Go back and grab the purple gourd.

Mechanics · Best use · Roles · Speed up

The core mechanic

The yellow button starts a real-time 30-minute countdown, not in-game. You don't have to stay — go do another purple puzzle while it runs, and the puzzle counts as solved the moment the timer hits zero. Come back to grab the gourd.

Best use

This is the only one of the 7 you can effectively 'set and forget'. Start it the moment you reach Challenge Ridge so the half hour runs in the background; by the time you get stuck on the other rooms, it's often already done. Don't leave the most time-efficient puzzle for last.

Roles

A single player can do it, but have one person own 'start it + come back to collect' while the others focus on the hard puzzles. Don't swap the host mid-puzzle, to avoid confusing the two rooms' progress.

Speed it up

Start it, then go straight to the 9 Speakers or 36 Drawings types that need back-and-forth; 30 minutes comfortably covers the tail of a couple of puzzles. Set an external timer so you remember to come collect it.

Why it won't work

Do I have to sit and wait 30 minutes?

No. The timer is real-time; it keeps running while you're elsewhere, and it'll be done by the time you return.

What if I quit the game midway?

Progress usually persists, but to be safe keep the game open and come back to collect after doing the others.
