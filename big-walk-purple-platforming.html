Platforming, Do It Fast

The same co-op platforming (stacking) as the main island, but with a strict timer and hourglasses to carry. The trick is 'get in position first, throw it as soon as you grab it'.

Before you start

  • Reached Challenge Ridge
  • At least 2 players who can stack

How to solve it

  1. 01

    Send one player to the rooftop

    Before grabbing the hourglass, get one player onto the first rooftop to wait.

  2. 02

    Throw the hourglass immediately

    The moment you pick it up, throw it toward your partner — don't hold it.

  3. 03

    Stack up

    After catching it, stack up to boost each other higher.

  4. 04

    Jump to the higher tier

    Aim for the next higher ledge, not the same level.

  5. 05

    Clear the zigzag gaps

    On the zigzag course, jump straight across the gaps.

  6. 06

    Take the right side

    The right side has wider landings — easier to stick.

Mechanics · Roles · Speed up

The core mechanic

The same co-op piggyback platforming as the main island, but with a strict countdown and an hourglass to carry. Falling or running out of time resets it, so smooth cooperation matters more than raw platforming skill.

Key: get in position before the hourglass

Before anyone picks up the hourglass, get one player up onto the first rooftop. The player who grabs it throws it straight to the teammate already up top, then stacks up and climbs to a higher tier — a higher ledge, not the same-level retry.

Roles

One player owns grab / pass the hourglass, the other owns the stack and the jump. The jumper reads the landing from ahead, and the instant they land turns back to take the hourglass so it doesn't sit on an unmovable platform.

Speed it up

On the zigzag course take the right-side landings, which are wider and stickier. The cardinal sin is pausing — better to be a beat slower and land clean. If you fall, don't panic; just restart from the nearest spawn.

Why it won't work

Running out of time?

The key is 'throw it as soon as you grab it + get in position early'. Don't stand around waiting.

Keep falling?

Take the right side with its wider landings instead of the shortest route.
