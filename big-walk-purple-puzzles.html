The 7 purple puzzles to the true ending

After the Big Goodbye ending, Challenge Ridge on the far east side unlocks 7 purple puzzles, each rewarding a purple gourd. The 7 purple gourds are a key step toward the Big Game true ending, but collecting them alone isn't enough — you also need the 8 red gourds, 15 total delivered to the green dome, before the white key rises. The full route to the true ending is in the section below.

Before you start

  • Finished the normal ending, Big Goodbye
  • Collected 5 red gourds to unlock the chairlift first
  • Several puzzles block voice chat — agree on hand signals / light signals first

How to solve it

  1. 01

    Finish the normal ending, then keep the same save

    Clear the Black Tower and the black sphere puzzles to trigger Big Goodbye; only then do the 7 purple puzzles unlock. They only appear now, and you must keep playing the same save — the black sphere stays broken open and the puzzles are only there now. Start a new world and it's wasted; go back to the old save.

  2. 02

    Ride the chairlift to the eastern ridge

    Take the chairlift east and get off at the ridge station.

  3. 03

    Turn right into the purple tunnel

    From the station, look right for a purple tunnel. Walk through it to reach Challenge Ridge, where all seven challenges are.

  4. 04

    Do the 7 puzzles in any order — but the gourds are only part of it

    There's no fixed order, and each one rewards a purple gourd. For the true ending you also need the 8 red gourds — 15 total delivered to the green dome near the train station / Map Room — before the white key rises. The full true-ending route is in the section below.

The 7 purple puzzles

  • Wait 30 Minutes HouseEasiest
    Enter the house and press the yellow button to start a 30-minute timer. You don't have to stay — go do other challenges. When the timer ends, come back to collect the purple gourd. Start it the moment you reach Challenge Ridge so the half hour runs in the background.
  • Golf · Heavy ObjectLongest, fiddly
    The hole is on Challenge Ridge, but the ball is at the golf course in the red area. Route: grab the ball in the red area → through the mountain pass to the green section → up and right to the rock by the chairlift. You can't throw or kick the ball — pass it back and forth (push the carrier to speed up). Prepare to exit the chairlift early, or a dropped ball loops the whole island (~10 min). Carry it through the purple tunnel, then up and right to the hole.
  • Light Wall · 16 LightsHard comms
    One player sees 16 lights; the other presses matching wall buttons in a different room, communicating only by light-bulb flashes. Number all 16 lights clockwise first so you share the same map, then relay zone numbers and button positions via flashes.
  • 9 Speakers, Can't TalkHardest
    One player listens to 9 sounds and communicates their positions to a partner using gestures only (no talking). Arrange the 9 into 3 rows — top 4, middle 4, bottom 1 — labeled left 1-4 / right 1-4 / middle. A top-left sound = raise left hand once; middle-row third = raise right hand three times. The listener signals positions; the other places the correct items.
  • 9 Symbols, Can't TalkSame idea
    Same mechanic as the 9 speakers, but with visual symbols instead of sounds. Use the same agreed gestures to tell your partner which symbol to pick. Before the restricted phase, inspect the fixed symbol set with your teammate and move clearly irrelevant pieces out of the working area.
  • 36 DrawingsVisual matching
    Pull all 36 drawing boards off the rack and spread them where both players can see. Many look alike — ask for details: 'filled or outline?' 'how many petals?' Sort into 'sure' and 'maybe' piles; if you fail, re-check the uncertain ones first.
  • Platforming, Do It FastTimed
    The same co-op platforming as the main island (stacking/jumping), but with a strict timer and hourglasses to carry. Get one player onto the first roof before grabbing the hourglass; throw it as soon as you pick it up, then stack. Jump to the next higher ledge (not the same level), clear the gaps on the zig-zag track, and take the right side where landings are wider.

True ending: Big Game

Continue the same save after the normal ending

The true ending is not a branching path, nor does it need a new run. Keep playing the same save after the normal ending: the black sphere stays broken open and the 7 purple puzzles only unlock now. If you already started a fresh world, that's wasted — go back to the old save.

Return to the Map Room and clear skipped normal puzzles

The 7 purple puzzles are not the whole story. Any normal puzzle skipped before the normal ending still counts toward 100%. Go back to the Map Room and look for empty flag poles on the 3D map, then finish each one — walking to a challenge point or leaving a mechanism half-operated does not count as complete.

Complete all 7 purple puzzles

Head to the far-east Challenge Ridge (unlocked only after the normal ending), pass through the purple tunnel, and finish all 7 purple puzzles — each awards a purple gourd. They're harder and slower than their main-line equivalents; solutions are in the list above and on each puzzle's own page.

Deliver all 15 gourds to the green dome building

The green dome building sits near the train station / Map Room, marked by a blue music-note icon on the folding map, and holds exactly 15 slots. Drop in all 8 red gourds + 7 purple gourds — note this is not the green tower that powers the chairlift, so don't mix them up. There's often a red pedestal with a yellow button nearby that opens a shortcut door through the archway.

Take the white key that rises at the start

Once all 15 slots are full and the world registers as fully complete, a white key rises near the spawn / tutorial area. The white key itself is a practical 'completeness' test: if it never rises, a puzzle or delivery is still outstanding — don't keep reloading or standing at the spawn point, go back and clean up.

Assign one permanent key carrier

As soon as the white key appears, hand it to one player to carry back to the start. Don't throw it, don't leave it by a train or chairlift, and don't pass it around the group — losing this key is far more costly than carrying it carefully.

Open the hidden room, read the message, walk through the white opening

In the tutorial area, on the wall where the cracked black sphere sits, is a hidden door that stayed locked through the main game. Insert the white key, climb the stairs into the quiet room, and sit through the short reading — it's about House House building the island while the players created the experience for one another. After it finishes, a bright white opening appears behind you; walk through and stay until the completion screen shows to unlock the true ending Big Game.

Why it won't work

How do I reach Challenge Ridge?

Finish the normal ending, ride the chairlift to the eastern ridge station, then turn right through the purple tunnel.

Chairlift won't let me on?

The chairlift unlocks after collecting 5 red gourds — check you have enough.

Dropped the golf ball on the chairlift?

It loops the whole island, about 10 minutes. Either wait it out or restart.

How do I trigger the true ending?

The 7 purple gourds are only part of it. The true ending needs 8 red + 7 purple — 15 total — all delivered to the green dome near the train station / Map Room; once all 15 slots are full, the white key rises at the start. Hand the white key to one player to carry back to the start and don't pass it around the group or set it down. Back in the tutorial area, insert it into the hidden door on the wall of the cracked black sphere and read through the message to trigger the Big Game ending.

I finished all 7 purple puzzles but still no true ending.

The purple puzzles are only part of 100%. Normal puzzles skipped before the normal ending and the 15-gourd delivery also count. The real check: has the white key risen? If it has, the world is complete; if not, something's still missing.
