Unlock the Green Tower and ride the chairlift

The chairlift is your fast route over the mountains, and it's locked behind the Green Tower. There are more than 5 puzzles in the region — finish any 5, collect the 5 gourd-shaped Heads, slot them into the rooftop Green Receiver, then run the blank key through 5 cutters to shape it and power the station. Riding it unlocks Big Sit and is the way to reach the Blue, Yellow, and Black Towers and the map's highest point (Big Climb).

Before you start

  • Cleared the Red Tower and unlocked the Map Room
  • Grab the Foldable Map from the Map Room first (pop it in a backpack) — several puzzles are located by coordinates and can't be read without it
  • At least 2 players — every puzzle needs coordination
  • Know the key-cutting mechanic

How to solve it

  1. 01

    See the rooftop Green Receiver first

    The Green Tower sits at (3930, 1900), and its roof holds a five-slot 'Green Receiver'. Scope out that end point before you start, so you know where every Head goes. The chairlift station is right beside it at (3950, 1950) — blue with a red floor and an empty key slot.

  2. 02

    Pick up the Foldable Map to read coordinates

    Several Green Tower puzzles are tied to coordinates (4166, 1899 and the beach houses). You can't read those without the paper map, so grab it from the Map Room first and pop it in a backpack — the map never shows your live position, so you still need the GPS or a compass to pin where you actually are.

  3. 03

    Solve any 5 nearby puzzles for 5 Heads

    The region has multiple puzzles, and any 5 reward a green gourd-shaped Head. There's no fixed order — if one stumps you, do another. More players only add switches or raise a target number; the solution stays the same. The 5 most commonly documented ones are below.

  4. 04

    Place the Heads in the rooftop Green Receiver

    Climb back to the roof and slot the 5 Heads into the receiver one at a time. Each slot lights up as it fills, and the fifth pops the key out of the back of the panel. Missing even one means no key.

  5. 05

    Run the key through 5 cutters

    What you get is a blank Green Key and it isn't usable yet. Start at the cutter by the Green Tower gate and follow each cutter's directional marker through all five until it's fully shaped. Shortest route: at the gate → pillar by the chairlift station → on the way to the chairlift → another station pillar → large rock by the chairlift.

  6. 06

    Power the chairlift station and ride

    Insert the finished key into the slot at the station (3950, 1950) and the chairlift powers on. Riding it unlocks Big Sit, and it opens up the Blue, Yellow, and Black Towers plus the map's highest point.

Green Tower puzzles (complete any 5)

  • 4166, 1899 coordinate switch4166, 1899
    Location: by the small train station near the broken-down train on the way to the tower, beside the red rail, with a board reading 4166, 1899 — that's a coordinate, not a password. One player stays by the locked box; the other travels to that coordinate, holds the hidden switch behind the rocks, and the box unlocks for the teammate waiting at it.
  • Orange Barn barrier shapes
    Location: beside the railway track, needs at least 2 players. Both press the buttons by the doors, which unlocks the collectible but also locks one player behind the barrier and cuts off voice chat. The trapped player's partner finds a hidden switch on a pillar to reveal a tile pattern; the trapped player must recreate it using only hand/face gestures and knocking on the barrier. Match it and the barrier drops.
  • Blue Signal head count
    Location: the green monkey-bar structure by the broken-down train. Activate the entrance switch to flash silhouettes of faces across several screens; spread out so every screen is visible to at least one player and nothing is missed. Add everyone's counts and enter the total on the blue panel. The count is random each attempt — don't copy the last run.
  • Yellow Stand lamps
    Location: a circular room on the way up to the Green Tower, full of yellow stands with a reset switch beside the locked collectible. Only activate stands showing a green star inside the glass; each correct pick fills a meter, and reaching 100% unlocks it. Hit a stand with a red bulb and you must reset from the switch. Stand positions are fixed, so drop throwable items (light bulbs) next to confirmed stands to stop re-checking.
  • Beach House tile count3259, 1883 / 3231, 1976
    The longest Green Tower puzzle, and it splits you up. Start at the red platform on the rocks below the tower, which shows two coordinates (3259, 1883 and 3231, 1976), and head north to the beach where you need one player in each of the two separate buildings. House A: the player downstairs activates the switch by the collectible to flash a set of images and counts them, then goes up to encode that count with tiles — Triangle = 10, Star = 5, Slash = 1 (for 21, place two triangles plus one slash) — and presses the button to raise the board. House B: the teammate uses a telescope in the other building to read the raised number and enters it into the console to finish.

Why it won't work

The Green Tower won't give a key?

Your 5 Heads aren't all placed. Check every one of the five slots on the rooftop receiver — even one empty stops the key.

The chairlift won't start?

Either the key isn't fully cut (you need all 5 cutters), or you're inserting it in the wrong place — it goes in the station slot at (3950, 1950), not the tower itself.

Solved a puzzle but got no Head?

Make sure the puzzle actually completed (celebration animation/sound) and that the collectible went back into the rooftop slots.

Can't read the coordinates?

Go back to the Map Room for the Foldable Map, and carry it in a backpack. Several Green Tower puzzles (4166, 1899 and the beach houses) are located by coordinates you can't see without it — just remember the map won't show where you're standing, so use the GPS or compass to pin yourself.

What is 4166, 1899?

It's a coordinate, not a password. One player holds the box while another walks to 4166, 1899 and presses the hidden switch behind the rocks — don't punch digits into the doors.

The Yellow Stand puzzle keeps resetting?

You hit a stand with a red bulb inside. Only press stands showing a green star; the positions are fixed, so mark confirmed stands and skip the re-checking.

Blue Signal count is wrong?

The number of faces shown is random each attempt. Make sure every screen is watched by at least one player, then total the counts.

Can't see the beach board from the other side?

It's too far without the telescope. Keep one player in each house — one builds the tile count, the other reads through the telescope and enters it on the console.

How do I reach the highest point (Big Climb)?

Ride the chairlift heading west, get off where the path crosses under the mountain, and walk up the small path on the left to the top for the Big Climb achievement.

How do I get Big Sit?

Once the chairlift is live, just ride it a trip — it's an easy grab.
