4166 / 1899 is coordinates, not a code

A plank beside a locked beach box reads "4166 · 1899," and inside sits a red gourd (a MacGuffin). There's no switch and no keyhole — which is exactly why so many players freeze. Those two numbers aren't a code to enter; they're map coordinates for somewhere else on the island. To read them you need to finish the Red Tower, grab the paper map from the Map Room, and bring at least two players.

Before you start

  • Complete the Red Tower: place 5 MacGuffins in its top console to earn a key
  • Cut/shape the key at the cutters
  • Open the Map Room next to spawn and take the paper map + compass from the table (the 3D island map on the floor has no coordinate grid — the paper map is the one with coordinates)
  • Optional: a GPS device near the Green Tower (ski lift) shows your live coordinates
  • At least 2 players: the button and the box are far apart

How to solve it

  1. 01

    Don't rush it — finish the Red Tower first

    You can't read the coordinates without a tool. Head to the Red Tower, place the 5 MacGuffins in the top console to earn a key, then cut the key at the cutters.

  2. 02

    Open the Map Room, get the paper map

    The cut key opens the locked room right next to spawn (some players call it the gym). The floor holds a large 3D map of the island with every MacGuffin and landmark marked, but it has no coordinate grid; the useful one is the foldable paper map + compass on the table. Pack it in a backpack before you head out.

  3. 03

    Read 4166 and 1899 on the paper map

    The map's edges are labeled with two-digit numbers — treat each as if it ends in 00: 41 reads 4100, 42 reads 4200, 19 reads 1900. So 4166 falls between the 41 (4100) and 42 (4200) gridlines, about two-thirds of the way across toward 42; 1899 sits just below the 19 (1900) line, right against it. Where the two lines cross is your target.

  4. 04

    Where to head

    The crossing lands in the south-central part of the island, between where the railway curves and where the ski lift vanishes into a tunnel, right by the train tracks. The tracks run past the spot, making a great landmark to follow and a fast route back.

  5. 05

    Split up: leave one player at the box

    The button and the box are far apart, so split the group first. Leave one player at the beach box — they grab the gourd the instant it opens, so they need to stay put.

  6. 06

    A second player finds the hidden button

    The traveler follows the paper map to the crossing, then searches the side of the rocks on the slope — there's a button camouflaged against the rock, easy to miss. Press and hold it. While it's held, the beach box opens and the waiting player grabs the gourd. The box only stays open while the button is pressed, so don't let go early — use walkie-talkies to time it.

Why it won't work

Why can't I solve it right away?

You don't have a tool that reads coordinates yet. The puzzle is a dead end until you finish the Red Tower and take the paper map from the Map Room — brute-forcing the beach spot early just wastes time.

Can I do this solo?

No. The button and the box are far apart, so one player can't hold the button and grab the gourd at the same time. You need at least two players at opposite ends.

I pressed it but the box didn't open.

You're probably at the wrong crossing. Double-check: 4166 should sit between the 41 (4100) and 42 (4200) gridlines, about two-thirds across; 1899 should be just below the 19 (1900) line. Don't wander — verify the crossing on the paper map.

I can't find the button.

It's camouflaged against the side of a rock, on the slope near the train tracks. Follow the railway to be sure, and search the south side of the "count the faces on the monitors" puzzle near the Green Tower.

The box keeps closing.

The box only stays open while the button is pressed. Don't let go until the player at the box confirms they have the gourd — walkie-talkies help here.

Is there a more reliable way to navigate?

The GPS device near the Green Tower (ski lift) shows your live coordinates, which is more accurate than estimating from the paper map and avoids misreading the gridlines.

What does the red gourd do?

It's a count item (MacGuffin / nubbin) that adds to the tower key it belongs to. Grabbing it from the beach box completes this puzzle.
