Unlock the Blue Tower and start the train

The three monorail trains are the fastest way around the island, and their key sits behind the Blue Tower — solve puzzles for 5 red gourds, place them at the top, cut the key, and power up the station. The trains never stop on their own: board one while it's moving and ride a stretch to unlock Big Ride (reaching the station or touching a parked carriage doesn't count).

Before you start

  • Ideally finish the Green Tower first — the chairlift reaches the Blue Tower's base, far faster than crossing the red drawbridge (not required, just much easier)
  • At least 2 players — several puzzles split the group between rooms
  • Know the key-cutting mechanic
  • Bring a walkie-talkie (for the separated rooms) and a light source — at night the blue beacon hides behind the ridge and the last cutters sit below the line of sight

How to solve it

  1. 01

    Find the Blue Tower (3540, 1850)

    The Blue Tower is on the eastern side of the island, a tall blue beacon with five slots at the top. Two routes: from the starting side, cross the red drawbridge and head up along the rail corridor; or open the Green Tower's chairlift, ride to the station under the highest point, then head down and climb up to the tower. Scout the five slots at the top first so you know where to return every gourd. If you pass a train station on the way, leave its controls alone — the whole Blue chain has to finish before it powers up.

  2. 02

    Solve any 5 puzzles for 5 red gourds

    Gourds are interchangeable — any 5 puzzles on the island work, not just the ones near the tower (guides also call them Heads). The most convenient ones are listed below, plus two alternates. Don't carry all your gear down the beach: return the first two or three to the tower as a checkpoint and learn the route, then solve the rest. When the last one is done, count the physical gourds in hand — a completed flag doesn't put the reward at the tower.

  3. 03

    Place them at the top for the blue key

    Slot the 5 gourds into the receiver at the top, one light per gourd; on the fifth, the tower hands you a key from its console. One short and it won't appear. This step only releases the key — it doesn't start the train. What you get is a blank key you can't use yet.

  4. 04

    Run the key through 5 cutters

    Start at the first cutter on the flat by the tower door: insert, wait for it to finish, pull it out, and follow the arrow on top to the next. Keep one player as the fixed key carrier and another with the light, so it doesn't slide off down a slope. The later cutters hide below rocks or ledges — if an arrow seems to point into open ground with no station in sight, look down near the last cutter before continuing; the easy-to-miss one sits by a large rock near the tower.

  5. 05

    Insert the key at the station (3380, 1740)

    Put the finished key into the train station control panel northwest of the tower and all three trains start circling the island clockwise. They never stop for you: board from the platform or higher ground as a carriage passes, or walk parallel to the track and hop aboard. Easiest is to have someone hold the lever in the first carriage. Ride a stretch to unlock Big Ride.

Blue Tower puzzles (complete any 5)

  • Turnstile Rooms Doodles3505, 1960
    Two players split into two rooms separated by turnstiles that cut normal chatter — bring a walkie-talkie. The left room holds the full set of doodle boards; the right room's wall shows the order of 5 of them. The left player describes each in turn (the shapes are deliberately vague — give each a short nickname and stick with it) while the right player hangs them in order; press the button under the black screen to confirm, and a correct answer drops the gourd. Turnstiles make you drop held items — pick them up on the way out.
  • 5-Minute Waiting Room3590, 2020
    Everyone enters the red shack, presses the two wall buttons to seal the door, and a 5-minute countdown starts. There's nothing else to solve — wait it out, talk to whoever is locked in with you, and collect the gourd at zero.
  • Obstacle Course3700, 1950
    1) Locked entrance: one player boosts the other over the door, who unblocks it from inside. 2) Green, blue and red blocks: boost one up, have the landing player grab and pull the other up, repeat. 3) Cross the winding red path together and press both buttons at the far end. 4) Split for the finish — one at the wind-up timer dispenser, the other across the gap by the green pot. 5) Throw the timer across, catch it and drop it in before it runs down.
  • Blindfolded Course3360, 1930
    One player wears the golden blindfold while a guide calls from outside. Before the mask goes on, have them walk the course once and note the first turn, the crouch passage, the first jump, the short staircase, the narrow approach and the final running jump. Use only short calls: forward, stop, turn left/right, crouch, stand, jump, run then jump. The last running jump is the main failure point — set them square on the platform first; a small left or right drift is enough to fall. Keep radios and idle chatter quiet so the guide's voice is the loudest thing there.
  • Charades Room3250, 1940
    The room separates the players who can see the target pose from the ones placing the answer. Don't memorise a fixed sequence — the order can change. Use a short vocabulary: crouch, neutral stand, both arms raised, left arm extended, right arm extended, one arm raised. The observer strikes one pose and waits; the answerer brings tiles to the window and the observer nods or jumps only when it matches — so you never lose track of which pose triggered a reset. With two teams, keep one permanent observer per side; rotating players mix up left and right or forget which pose already passed.
  • Green Flare (alternate)
    One player stays on the launch platform and fires the green flare; everyone else watches where it lands and walks over. A locked box waits at the landing point. Once someone stands at the box, signal the platform player to press the button and the lid opens — take the gourd. The handoff across open terrain is far easier over a walkie-talkie.
  • Headphone and Chair (alternate)
    A two-location puzzle: one player sits in the green chair overlooking the shoreline; the other must be standing at a locked box on the far side. Head northwest of the Green Tower until the turquoise radio station comes into view — the box is in the rocks beside it. With someone in place, the seated player presses the button and the box pops open.

Using the train after you unlock it

Three trains run a clockwise loop with stations all over the island

Activating the station kick-starts a whole rail loop, not one car — several trains circulate clockwise past a spread of stations. A timetable on each platform shows how far away the next train is, so you can time a jump instead of standing around. One of the trains also runs through the tunnel below the Blue Tower's station.

Built for co-op: walkie-talkie and radio on board

The carriages carry a walkie-talkie and a radio receiver, so you can talk to the other riders and to anyone else on the island holding a walkie-talkie. There's a horn and a speed lever too.

You can pause it, but only while you hold it

The first carriage has a lever that holds the train still — but only while someone keeps it pressed. Let go and it moves on, so you can't just park it and wander off. If you're waiting on a straggler, assign one player to hold it.

It never stops for you — board while it's moving

The trains don't pull in and dwell. Jump on from the platform or a higher spot as it goes by, or walk parallel to the track and hop aboard. The hassle-free way is to hold the lever to bring it to a stop, then board one by one.

Big Ride requires riding a moving train

The achievement wants you to actually ride a train that's in motion — reaching the station, touching a parked carriage, or standing on the rails doesn't count. Wait for it to circulate, hop aboard, and stay on for a stretch; it usually pops on the first ride.

Treat it as transport, not a completion flag

The train is a way to get around, not proof the island is done. After the first loop, go back to the Map Room and check which flags are still empty, then push on to the Black Tower, the last radio, and the ending.

Why it won't work

The Blue Tower won't give a key?

Your 5 gourds aren't all placed. Check each slot at the top — even one empty stops the key. Gourds are interchangeable, so don't grind one puzzle: solve a different one and bring that reward back.

The train won't move?

Either the key isn't fully cut or you're inserting it in the wrong spot — it goes in the station controller northwest of the tower, not the tower itself. If it goes in but won't turn, the cutting isn't done; if it won't go in, wrong terminal.

Can't get on the moving train?

It never stops. Board from the platform or higher ground as it passes, or walk parallel and hop aboard; the reliable way is to have someone hold the lever in the first carriage to stop it, then climb on one by one.

Big Ride didn't pop even though I'm on the train?

It has to be a train that's moving. Standing in the station or riding a parked carriage doesn't count — wait for it to run and ride a stretch.

Turnstile puzzle keeps dropping items?

Turnstiles make you drop whatever you're holding. Pick things back up on the way out, and don't juggle extra items while passing through.

Key vanished on a slope?

Stop and check the ground below the carrier — the key slides farther than a person. Never throw or kick it; if it goes missing, first confirm who's actually holding it.

Cutter arrow points into empty terrain?

You've probably missed a hidden one. When the arrow seems to overshoot, scan the nearest rocks and the ledge below the last cutter before moving on; the easy-to-miss station sits by a large rock near the tower.

Activated the train — now what?

It's transport, not progress. Return to the Map Room and use the flags to see what's left, then push the Black Tower and collect the last radio.
