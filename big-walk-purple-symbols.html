9 Symbols, Can't Talk

Identical mechanic to the 9 Speakers, just with visual symbols instead of sounds. Reuse the same gesture system from that puzzle.

Before you start

  • Reached Challenge Ridge
  • At least 2 players — voice is blocked, so agree on gestures first

How to solve it

  1. 01

    Arrange the 9 into 3 rows

    Top 4, middle 4, bottom 1 — same labeling as the 9 Speakers.

  2. 02

    Agree on gestures

    Reuse the same gesture system.

  3. 03

    One player reads the symbols

    The reader determines which position the target symbol is in.

  4. 04

    Signal the position

    Relay the row/column position to the partner.

  5. 05

    The other picks the symbol

    The partner selects the matching symbol, aligning them to finish.

Mechanics · Numbering · Roles · Speed up

The core mechanic

Identical to the 9 Speakers, just with visual symbols instead of sounds: one player sees the target symbol and signs which one to choose; the blocked-voice rule is the same. Reuse the exact numbering and gestures from the Speakers puzzle.

Reuse the numbering & gestures

Keep the same 3-row layout (top 4, middle 4, bottom 1) and gestures from the 9 Speakers. If your teammate just finished that one, reuse the agreement directly and skip re-negotiating it.

Roles

The reader calls 'which row / column + which symbol', and the operator places the matching symbol in position. When symbols look alike, include detail in the call (outline vs filled, orientation).

Speed it up

Before the restricted phase, look over the fixed symbol set together and move obviously irrelevant pieces out of the working area to cut mis-taps. Call slowly and don't rush — better to over-call than mis-call.

Why it won't work

Getting it mixed up with the last puzzle?

They share the same mechanic, so after finishing the Speakers this one feels much smoother.
