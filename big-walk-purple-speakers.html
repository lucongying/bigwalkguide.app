9 Speakers, Can't Talk

Widely considered the hardest. One player listens to 9 sounds and signals their positions to a partner using gestures only — no talking. The gesture system is everything.

Before you start

  • Reached Challenge Ridge
  • At least 2 players — voice is blocked, so agree on gestures first

How to solve it

  1. 01

    Arrange the 9 into 3 rows

    Top 4, middle 4, bottom 1, labeled 'left 1-4', 'right 1-4', and 'middle'.

  2. 02

    Agree on gestures

    A top-left sound = raise the left hand once; middle-row third = raise the right hand three times, and so on.

  3. 03

    One player listens

    The listener determines which position each sound comes from.

  4. 04

    Signal the position

    Relay the row/column position to the partner via gestures.

  5. 05

    The other places the item

    The partner grabs and places the correct items, aligning them to finish.

Mechanics · Numbering · Roles · Speed up

The core mechanic

One player hears 9 sounds and judges where each comes from, while the other places items in the right spot; no talking, gestures only. Widely the hardest of the 7, hard specifically because translating 'where I heard it' into a readable gesture takes practice.

Lay the 9 out as 3 rows

Give the 9 positions a stable coordinate system first: 3 rows (top 4, middle 4, bottom 1) with left / middle / right marked. E.g. 'top-left' = raise the left hand once; 'third in the middle row' = raise the right hand three times.

Roles

The listener hears and signs the grid position; the partner places the correct item there. The caller should fully hear the sound before signing — don't gesture mid-hear. The placer sets the item down and confirms it once before moving on.

Speed it up

Run through every grid's gesture with your teammate before you enter the blocked zone, confirm it, then start. Count with one hand's fingers (1–4) and pause to signal 'I'm about to sign' before you sign. Headphones help tell left from right.

Why it won't work

Gestures getting mixed up?

Repeat slowly a couple of times and confirm the partner understands before placing.

Can't tell the direction?

Wear headphones — stereo sound helps you tell left from right.
